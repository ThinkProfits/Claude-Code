# Two new Grok bots (Francis approved, 26 Nov)

- SEO & AI Briefing: sends Francis a daily briefing at about 2 PM Manila, covering SEO, Google updates and AI tools he can actually use in his work.
- Analytics & Search Data: the go-to bot for Google Search Console, GA4 and Semrush data (traffic and ranking drops, indexing, URL Inspection, numbers for reports). It's read-only by default and requests indexing only when it's approved. It goes through the chain: QA Reviewer, then The Man, then Francis.

The team is organised by job, not one bot per tool: Citations Builder does citations and BrightLocal, GBP Manager does Business Profiles, Local SEO Strategist does content and strategy, and QA Reviewer does checks. Briefs about GSC or GA4 can name Bot: Analytics & Search Data.
