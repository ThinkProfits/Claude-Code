For a low-authority domain, lead SEO strategy recommendations with strengthening existing
core/commercial pages (especially ones already showing some traction, like real impressions
at a poor position) before proposing a batch of new niche/long-tail pages.

**Why:** On a Q4 2026 quarterly plan, Francis built a plan centred on six new niche pages
plus bio meta-description cleanup. The client's manager rejected this direction, calling it
UX/technical-adjacent rather than core strategy, and redirected focus to the site's existing
core service pages plus fixing the booking page. This matched the underlying data: the
domain had near-zero authority (Semrush Domain Rank ~15M) and one core page was already
earning thousands of impressions at a poor position, a much higher-leverage target than
starting new pages from zero.

**How to apply:** When auditing a low-authority site and asked for new page/content
recommendations, check first whether existing pages are under-optimized or already earning
impressions/traffic at a bad position (via GSC data) before recommending new pages to build.
Surface "strengthen what's already getting visibility" as the lead recommendation, with new
niche pages framed as backlog/next-quarter — not as competing with core-page work in the same
quarter. Also worth checking early: whether a service business's site architecture assumption
(e.g., "online only" vs. "online + in-person") is actually confirmed with the client before
building page structure around it.
