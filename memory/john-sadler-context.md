**Service areas (priority order):** South Surrey, White Rock, Cloverdale, Langley, Walnut Grove, Crescent Beach, Ocean Park, Morgan Creek, Grandview Heights, Elgin, Brookswood, Murrayville, North Delta. **No longer serving Abbotsford** (dropped as of the Sept 2026 doc) - any Abbotsford-targeted content/keywords/ads should be flagged for removal.

**3 tracked SEO markets:** Surrey (strongest), White Rock (mid-tier), Langley (weakest - biggest growth opportunity). Semrush Position Tracking project: "John Sadler", project_id 2824033, tracked URL mask `*.johnsadler.ca/*`.

**Services offered:** hot water tank install/replace, tankless/Navien install+repair+maintenance, boiler install/repair/maintenance (incl. combi), furnace install/repair/maintenance, heat pump install (+ **repair newly offered mid-2026**, early stage), AC install (+ **repair newly offered mid-2026**), gas fitting, in-floor/radiant heating, strata maintenance (up to 4-storey residential, treated as residential not commercial).

**NOT offered** (don't write content/ads/track keywords implying these): general plumbing repairs (leaky faucets/toilets), drain cleaning (subbed out), new construction, propane/oil work or conversions, fire installations, commercial work (stratas are the one exception).

**Navien is the flagship brand differentiator** - certified specialist, known nationally. Navien+location keyword combos return ~zero CA search volume in Semrush; the doc's own SEO notes say to prefer **category-level Navien tracking** (e.g. "navien error codes") over per-location Navien terms. As of Sept 2026 audit, "navien tankless repair" and "navien tankless water heater repair" (720 CA volume each) already rank ~#10-14 organically on /services/navien/ but were completely absent from Position Tracking - worth checking this stays fixed.

**Approvals:** Tina Sadler is primary approver for all content/pages, reviews first then loops in Colin for technical fact-checking. Never publish without client sign-off; review queue can sit for a while, follow up proactively.

**Brand constraints:** No discount/gimmick marketing, no fake urgency, no inflated-then-discounted pricing. Anniversary/achievement promos (50th anniversary is a 2026 milestone) should be community/charity-focused, not sales-driven. **Zero AI-generated images or fake personas for this account, under any circumstances** - Colin explicitly flagged "AI slop" concern. No em dashes in content (use brackets/commas/colons/semicolons instead). FAQ schema must stay clean - no links or branded language inside FAQ entries.
