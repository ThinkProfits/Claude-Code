When replying to a client's monthly report thread in Slack (the automated review Andrew's report processor posts in each client channel around the 7th), reply with a couple of bullet points: what the report showed that matters, what has been done because of it, and what is planned next (with who and roughly when). A bare "I'm on this" / "I've got this covered" is not enough.

**Why:** Andrew, Weekly Team Check-in 2026-11-17 (01:29:16 in the Gemini transcript): the replies so far had no context. Detail lets Andrew, Shawn and Andrew's Claude bots see what's been done, and makes sure big follow-ups ("it needs seven new pages and it's going to be a few months") don't get forgotten.

**How to apply:**
- Every client monthly report, every month. Short is fine, but name the specific actions.
- Split "Done" and "Next" when both exist; name the owner when it isn't you.
- Long fixes (multi-month) get their own line so they're tracked and followed up.
- SEO only. No Google Ads, PPC or LSA lines in report replies, even when the review has a PPC section (Francis, 2026-11-19).
- No "Waiting on client:" lines in report replies, for any client (Francis, 2026-11-19). Client blockers stay in CARD.md, not in the Slack reply.
- Wording per client follows the client's rules in clients/<client>/CLIENT.md or CARD.md.
