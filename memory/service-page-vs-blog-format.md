Francis rejected a service-page draft (2026-09-30) because it read like a blog post rather than a service page. The draft had explainer H2s ("What Is X", "X vs. Y"), long paragraphs, and a docx labelled "Blog Deliverable".

**Why:** Service pages are conversion pages. Buyers scan them for what's included, proof and the next step. Educational explainers belong in blogs or the FAQ.

**How to apply:** For any service or landing page, use this structure:
- a hero (H1, a subheadline, 1–2 lines of copy, buttons and a trust strip)
- a who-we-serve list
- short H3 service blocks, 1–2 sentences each
- a numbered how-it-works process
- a why-choose-us proof list
- a cross-sell links block
- a short FAQ (question-keyword definitions go here, not in H2s)
- CTAs after the major sections

Add developer layout notes (cards, grid, accordion). Use the live site's own wording for service details wherever possible. Always label and lay out the docx as a Page deliverable, not a Blog. Related: [[page-update-doc-format]].
