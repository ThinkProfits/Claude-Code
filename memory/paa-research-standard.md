When doing keyword or content research for any page/blog topic (any client), pull People Also Ask / "phrase_questions"-style SERP question data for the target topic, not just head-term volume and keyword difficulty. Use these questions to:
- Populate or expand the page's FAQ section (and its FAQPage schema, when present).
- Surface content-gap ideas for H2/H3 sections the current draft is missing.

Semrush MCP: `phrase_questions` report (via `keyword_research` toolkit) returns question-form queries with volume for a seed phrase — this is the practical PAA proxy available in this workspace.

**Why:** Francis asked (2026-09-24, during a content audit) for PAA questions to become a standard part of research and content writing, not a one-off.
**How to apply:** Add a PAA/question-research step to keyword research before drafting or auditing any page or blog post: [[agency-blog-writer]] work and any ThinkProfits SEO audit or content brief. Cross-check questions against what the draft's FAQ already covers before adding — don't duplicate.
