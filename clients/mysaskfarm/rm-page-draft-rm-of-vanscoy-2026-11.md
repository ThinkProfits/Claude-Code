## What farmland sells for in the RM of Vanscoy

Vanscoy land is priced first on what it grows. Location counts too: Highway 7 is twinned all the way from Saskatoon to Delisle, and distance to the city and to a good haul road is one of the things buyers weigh.

What moves the price on a Vanscoy quarter:

- **Soil and productivity:** soil class, stones, slope, drainage and the land's cropping history
- **Distance to Saskatoon and Highway 7:** how far, and how easy, the haul is
- **Crop land vs. pasture:** cultivated acres and pasture sell to different buyers at very different prices
- **Buildings:** a yard site with a home, shop or bins adds value on top of the land itself

### Assessed value vs. market value

Your SAMA assessment is not a sale price. It values land on its productive capacity as of January 1, 2023, and that base date stays in place through 2028, so on most Vanscoy quarters the assessment now trails what land actually sells for.

## Vanscoy crop yields

Over the 10 years from 2016 to 2025, Vanscoy's average yields matched or beat the RM of Corman Park next door for every major crop. The clearest gaps were in spring wheat (46.4 bushels an acre against 39.1) and lentils.

Moisture still sets the year. The 2021 drought cut Vanscoy canola to 13.7 bushels an acre, while 2025 came in at 54.9, the RM's best canola crop of the decade.

### RM of Vanscoy No. 345 crop yields: 2025 vs. 10-year average (2016 to 2025)

- **Canola:** 54.9 bu/ac in 2025; 36.8 bu/ac average
- **Spring wheat:** 65.0 bu/ac in 2025; 46.4 bu/ac average
- **Barley:** 91.9 bu/ac in 2025; 61.4 bu/ac average
- **Oats:** 128.7 bu/ac in 2025; 74.3 bu/ac average
- **Field peas:** 59.0 bu/ac in 2025; 39.1 bu/ac average
- **Lentils:** 2,816 lb/ac in 2025; 1,664 lb/ac average

Source: [Government of Saskatchewan, RM Yields data (Ministry of Agriculture Crop Report and Saskatchewan Crop Insurance Corporation)](https://dashboard.saskatchewan.ca/agriculture/rm-yields/rm-yields-data).

## FAQs About the RM of Vanscoy

### Where is the RM of Vanscoy?

The RM of Vanscoy No. 345 is in central Saskatchewan, just southwest of Saskatoon. Highway 7 runs through it, twinned from Saskatoon to Delisle, and the RM office is in the village of Vanscoy, which the highway now bypasses. The RM covers 861 km² of farmland and rural roads.

### What towns are in the RM of Vanscoy?

The towns of Delisle and Asquith and the village of Vanscoy sit inside the RM's boundaries `[VERIFY]`. Each has its own council, while the RM looks after the farmland and rural roads around them. The RM's office is in Vanscoy.

### How big is the RM of Vanscoy?

The RM of Vanscoy covers 861.1 km², roughly 330 square miles or about 330 sections of land. It had 2,799 residents in the 2021 Census, down slightly from 2,840 in 2016. It's part of Census Division No. 12, west of the city of Saskatoon.
