## What's wrong with the current page

- The H1 is "Sell with US" and doesn't mention farmland or Saskatchewan. The page averages position 3.5 on its few impressions (GSC, Jul to Oct 2026), so a keyword-led rewrite has room to grow.
- It says "Farm Home or Land Parcel" seven times. Farm homes read as residential, which is out of scope (CLIENT.md section 2).
- "More than 80% of buyers checking the web first" has no source. We can't cite it, so it comes out.
- "Ensure you get the best price" reads as a promise. We can't make one (no price or sale guarantees).
- "Network ... throughout the nation" is vague, and the brokerage isn't named on the page.
- There's no process, no FAQ and no proof of Grant's farming background, which is his strongest selling point.
