## What farmland sells for in the RM of Blucher

Blucher land is priced first on what it grows, and then on access. The RM is served by four primary weight highways and both CN and CP rail lines, so how far a quarter sits from Saskatoon and from a good haul road is part of what buyers weigh.

What moves the price on a Blucher quarter:

- **Soil and productivity:** soil class, stones, slope, drainage and the land's cropping history
- **Distance to Saskatoon:** how far the quarter is from the city and its services
- **Highway access:** a quarter close to Highway 5 or 16 is easier to haul from than one at the end of a long grid road
- **Size and shape:** a full, square quarter draws a different buyer than a small or awkwardly shaped parcel

### Assessed value vs. market value

A SAMA assessment is a tax number, not a sale price. It's based on the land's productive capacity as of January 1, 2023, and stays in place through 2028, so with values up every year since, most assessments now trail what land actually sells for.

## Blucher crop yields

Canola, wheat, barley, oats, peas and lentils all show up in Blucher's crop reports. The RM's best canola crop of the last decade came in 2025 at 46.3 bushels an acre, just ahead of 2017.

Dry years show up fast. In the 2021 drought, Blucher canola fell to 12.9 bushels an acre and spring wheat to 22, which is why the 10-year average says more about the land than any single season.

## FAQs About the RM of Blucher

### Where is the RM of Blucher?

The RM of Blucher No. 343 is in central Saskatchewan, starting about 10 minutes southeast of Saskatoon. It takes in the farmland around Clavet, Bradwell and Allan, and Highways 5 and 16 both cross it. The RM office is at 34 Struan Street in Bradwell.

### What is the RM of Blucher known for?

Farming and potash. Along with grain and oilseed land, the RM's economy includes Nutrien's Allan and Patience Lake potash mines, Nutrien's Clavet fertilizer depot and Cargill's Clavet facility. It's also served by both CN and CP rail lines and four primary weight highways.

### What communities are in the RM of Blucher?

The town of Allan and the villages of Bradwell and Clavet sit inside the RM, each with its own council `[VERIFY]`. The RM looks after the farmland and rural roads around them. It covers 789 km² and had 1,984 residents in the 2021 Census.
