## H2: What a Farmer Sees in a Piece of Land

When I walk a parcel, I'm not just counting acres. I'm looking at:

- **Soil class and productivity**, and what the land has actually grown
- **Moisture and drainage**, including the low spots that hold water in a wet spring
- **The RM**, its roads, access and what nearby land has sold for
- **Stones, bush and fence lines**, the things that change what a buyer will pay
- **Buildings, bins and yard sites**, and whether they add value or cost

A buyer who farms will notice all of it. Your listing should too. [GRANT: one short real example, no names, where your farm knowledge changed the price or the sale.]
