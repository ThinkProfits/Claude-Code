What farmland sells for in Corman Park

Quick answer: no single number fits the whole RM. Land in Corman Park is priced on two things at once: what it can grow, and how close it sits to Saskatoon. A quarter of good grain land a few miles from the city can carry development value that a similar quarter near the RM's outer edge won't.

Across Saskatchewan, farmland values rose 9.4% in 2025, the 13th straight year of gains, according to Farm Credit Canada's 2025 Farmland Values Report (released March 24, 2026). Growth varied by region, from about 12% in the northeast, northwest and east-central regions to 4.8% in west-central Saskatchewan. Corman Park falls in FCC's [region] region.

Assessed value vs. market value

Your SAMA assessment isn't a sale price. SAMA values farmland from a productivity model (soil and expected wheat yield) and revalues every four years. The 2025 revaluation uses a January 1, 2023 base date, so assessments usually trail what land actually sells for, especially after the gains of the last few years.

Land, soil and crop yields

Corman Park is mostly Dark Brown soil country: solid grain and oilseed land, with lighter and sandier ground along the South Saskatchewan River valley and pasture where the land breaks. Canola, spring wheat, durum, barley, oats and peas are all grown here.

Yields swing with moisture. The 2021 drought cut Corman Park canola to 10.7 bu/ac, while 2025 came in at 46.2 bu/ac. That's why the 10-year average says more about the land than any single year.

Corman Park farmland: common questions

Is there farmland for sale in the RM of Corman Park?

Yes. Listings change weekly, so the current list is at the top of this page. On October 22, 2026 it showed 13 land and farm listings in the RM, asking from $699,900 to $5,000,000.

What does an acre of farmland sell for in Corman Park?

It depends on soil class, size, access and distance to Saskatoon. Land on the city edge can carry development value on top of its farm value. For a figure on a specific parcel, ask for a free evaluation.

What soil zone is the RM of Corman Park in?

Most of the RM sits in Saskatchewan's Dark Brown soil zone.

How is farmland assessed in Corman Park?

SAMA sets assessments from a productivity model based on soil and expected wheat yield, revalued every four years. The 2025 revaluation uses a January 1, 2023 base date, so assessed value is usually well below market value.

How long does farmland take to sell in Corman Park?

It varies with price, parcel size and season. Winter (November to March) is the busiest time for farmland deals in Saskatchewan. See the best time to sell farmland in Saskatchewan.

How do I contact the RM of Corman Park office?

For taxes, permits, zoning and council business, contact the RM directly through rmcormanpark.ca. For buying or selling land, contact Grant.
