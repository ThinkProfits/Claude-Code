Hi sir @Andrew Silbernagel, sorry for the long delay on this one. Here's what I found on the GSC YoY drop:

*Short version:* almost all of it is one page. The Trailers & RV pumping page went from 706 clicks in Jul–Sep 2025 to 74 in Jul–Sep 2026. That's 632 of the 649 clicks the site lost (97%). Take that page out and the rest of the site is flat YoY (553 vs 536 clicks, -3%; July alone is +7%).

*What happened to the RV page*
• It was steady at 55–80 clicks a week until early Sept 2025, then slid to about 10 a week by November, and summer 2026 never recovered, so it's not just seasonality.
• It lost the whole "RV pump-out near me" query group (rv tank pumping service near me, rv septic pumping, mobile rv pumping, etc.). We had positions 6–15 last year and now 0 impressions.
• It's indexed and crawled fine (Oct 17), and the content didn't change before the drop (Wayback copies from Jul 31 and Oct 8, 2025 match). The timing lines up with Google's August 2025 spam update (Aug 26 – Sep 22).
• Some of that "near me" demand looks like it moved to the map pack. Clicks through the GBP link went from 14 to 120 YoY.

*Other notes*
• Impressions and avg position aren't comparable YoY. Google dropped the num=100 parameter in Sept 2025, which cut deep-position impressions in GSC for most sites. So I'd judge this one on clicks only.
• Small real losses on pump chambers, grease traps and the septic hub (about 30 clicks combined), which fits the septic cannibalization issue. Portable toilets and storm drains grew.

*What I'd suggest*
1. Rework the RV page as a proper service page: fix the H1 typo ("RVTank"), local title/meta, remove the DIY pumping section, FAQ accordion, internal links from the homepage and services page.
2. Check who now ranks for the RV terms once Semrush units are back.
3. Confirm RV pump-out is listed as a GBP service, and push RV posts May–Sept.
4. Rule out a manual action in GSC.
5. Note in the monthly report that pre-Sept 2025 impressions aren't comparable.

Let me know if you want me to go ahead with the RV page rework, and I'll send the changes in a doc first.
