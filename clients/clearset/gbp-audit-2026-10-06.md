## Headline findings

1. **527 services listed, roughly 450 of them not offered.** About 50 auto-suggested services were bulk-added to every category, including waterjet cutting for aerospace parts, water bill payment, dumpster rental, asbestos removal, pump motor rewinding, and India-specific items ("Sintex tank cleaning", "Pre-monsoon tank cleaning", "Overhead water tank cleaning").
2. **Some services contradict the website.** GBP lists "Septic system inspection", "Real estate septic inspection", "Septic tank certification" and "Septic tank inspection". The homepage FAQ says Clearset does **not** do septic inspections (licensed plumber/ROWP only). This is a customer-trust and GBP-guideline risk (services must be ones you actually provide).
3. **Three wrong categories.** "Water jet cutting service" (industrial cutting, not hydro jetting), "Water pump supplier" (Clearset doesn't sell pumps), "Water utility company" (Clearset isn't a utility). Removing them deletes 154 junk services in one step.
4. **"Portable toilet supplier" is already a secondary category.** Good. The portable sanitation gap is on the website and in the services list, not the categories.
5. **Price mismatch.** The GBP service "Toilet rental per month for once a week cleaning" shows $140. The website says from $165/month.
6. **Duplicate/vague custom services** under the primary category: "Vac Truck Services" + "Vacuum Truck Services", "Water Hauling" + "Water Delivery" + "Water Supply Services", "Residential Services", "Storage Tanks", "Tank Maintenance", "Septic Tank Problems", "24hr Emergency Services".
7. **Description** claims "Health Canada compliant" (unverified, flag for client), uses a fixed rating ("4.6/5") that will go stale, says "Metro Vancouver & the Lower Mainland" (site says Metro Vancouver & the Fraser Valley), and never says "portable sanitation".
8. **Citation inconsistencies seen in search results (separate task).** Facebook, Instagram, Yelp and coquitlamlocal.ca show 778-825-1032, while the website shows (778) 907-9875. Maptons lists an old address (615 Laurier Ave). Confirm which phone is current and clean up via BrightLocal Citation Tracker.
