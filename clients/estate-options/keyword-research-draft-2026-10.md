**Finding:** Andrew's instinct holds up. None of her alternative names gets meaningful search volume. The individual document names (wills, power of attorney, personal directive) carry the demand, and "estate planning" is the only *umbrella* term people actually search.

**Recommendation for her to choose from:**
1. **Preferred:** H1 "Wills & Estate Planning", with this clarifier near the top: "Wills, enduring powers of attorney and personal directives. We don't provide tax planning or trusts, and we're happy to work alongside your accountant or tax lawyer." This captures the search demand, answers her tax-planning concern head-on, and screens out enquiries she doesn't want.
2. **Compromise:** H1 "Wills, Powers of Attorney & Personal Directives", with "estate planning" used in the title tag, meta description and body copy. We'd lose some umbrella visibility, but the component terms still carry the page.
3. "Will & Incapacity Planning" and "Personal Planning Documents" have **no measurable Calgary demand**. Not recommended as the page name.

### The gaps that matter most

1. **No Calgary law firm we found offers to act as executor or attorney on a dedicated page.**
   - The closest are articles *about* executors (West Legal, Kahane, Getz Collins).
   - West Legal's article says the firm likely won't act as executor.
   - The only "we act as executor" pages are non-lawyer businesses (Leading Edge Executor Services, Financial Concierge) and trust companies.
   - That makes it her clearest point of difference.
2. **Mediation as an estate service** is rare among the sites we checked. Her QMed is a differentiator.
3. **Home visits:** only Housecall Wills and Kahane push this. Lead with it.
4. **FAQ blocks and question-format headings** are thin on most competitor service pages. Her client-FAQ list fills this gap directly, and it's what Google's AI Overviews and other AI answers tend to pull from.
5. **Credentials over badges:** competitors lean on Best Lawyers and review counts. She has no testimonials (by choice), so her bio, teaching history and process have to carry the trust.

### Compliance watch-outs found on competitor sites (don't copy)
- "specializing" or "specialty": Housecall Wills, West Legal, MacDonald Law North
- "expert" or "experts": Underwood Gilholme, Bruni, Kirk Montoute Dawson, Vest
- Our copy uses "practises wills and estates exclusively", and avoids "expert" and "best" as well, to be safe.

## 5. What Munro & Crawford's data tells us

M&C is in Vancouver, so their volumes don't carry over to Calgary. Their *patterns* do:

- **The homepage can win the main "estate lawyer [city]" term.** Their homepage ranks about #7 for "estate lawyer vancouver" (GSC, 836 impressions in 90 days).
  - **Lesson:** give Home a single clear target. Don't build a separate "estate lawyers" page that competes with it, which is the cannibalization problem M&C now has between their homepage, /estate-lawyers-vancouver/ and /wills-and-estate-planning/.
- **Probate is their strongest service term:** "probate lawyer vancouver" averages about #7, and probate ad groups had the best cost per conversion (July 2026).
  - This supports making Estates & Probate a priority page, along with probate content for Google Ads.
- **Their professional-executor page brings in real clicks:** about 52 in 90 days at roughly #12, despite low volume. That's evidence her Executor Services page can work.
- **Their biggest traffic comes from blogs, not service pages:**
  - "what is a power of attorney" (31k impressions)
  - probate fees
  - how long it takes to settle an estate
  - avoiding estate tax
  - Her top-FAQ list maps directly onto these. That makes them a ready-made 6-month blog roadmap (Alberta versions).
- **Fees FAQ:** she already likes theirs. Their "many services are fixed fee; contact us for the schedule" pattern fits her "rates vary" stance.
