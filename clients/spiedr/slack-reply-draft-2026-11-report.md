Read the PDF and the summary. I dug into GSC to see how much of this is real. Short version: most of it is seasonal, a few service pages have genuinely slipped, and the key events drop has a known cause.

**Traffic: mostly the end of fire season**
From August to October, impressions fell 40 to 85% on almost every page while average positions barely moved. The Mark 3 QS product page is a good example: position 5.6 to 5.4, impressions down 54%. That's demand, not rankings. Year over year, the October blogs are carrying weight: the prevention vs suppression post brought in 36 clicks (the top page on the site), and the liability and zombie fires posts added 14 more. None of those existed last October.

**Rankings: a few service pages slipped for real (October 2026 vs October 2025, GSC average position)**

- Equipment rentals: 11.1 to 22.1 (impressions down 75%)
- Wildfire consulting: 13.8 to 26.4
- Sprinkler kits: 11.1 (August) to 21.0
- Structure protection sprinklers: 27.2 (August) to 35.8

One correction to the summary: the Structural Protection Unit page looks like it fell from 5 to 18, but its core terms are holding ("structure protection unit" at #2, "structural protection unit" at #9). The average is being dragged down by broad queries it now shows for at #60+, like "civil structure protection". Same reporting quirk as last month.

The consulting and rentals pages are two of the URLs I fixed on Sep 17 (canonical pointing to a redirect, and missing from the sitemap). I'd expect some wobble while Google settles them, but I'll check they're indexed under the right URL.
