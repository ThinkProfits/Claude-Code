Confirming I've read the PDF report and reviewed the automated summary. I dug into the two SEO items and have a few corrections plus one new issue.

**Top 3 / Top 10 drop-offs — mostly a reporting artifact**

"Structural fire protection" didn't really fall 13 spots. Daily data shows it held position 6–7 for 29 of the 31 days in August and only touched 19 on the last two days. It's back at 7 as of 16 September. Same pattern for "structural fire protections systems." Because the report compares 1 Aug to 31 Aug, a two-day wobble reads as a collapse. Both keywords point at `/structural-protection-unit/`, which is well linked and holding #1 on its primary term — nothing to fix.

Three declines are real and haven't recovered:
- *top wildfire sprinkler protection* — 3 → 9 → 14, still drifting
- *fire protection roof sprinkler system* — 8 → 13, flat since mid-August
- *fire fighting pumps for sale* — 9 → 18, volatile

All three land on pages with weak or zero internal link support, which is the cheapest thing to test first.

**New since the report — worth telling the client**

Three keywords that were stable or #1 moved sharply around 11–12 September: "portable fire pumps canada" (1 → 16), "wildland fire structure protection" (1 → 7), and "wildland fire pumps" (3 → 21). Three unrelated terms moving on the same two days points to a SERP-side event rather than anything on the site. I'd monitor and report next cycle rather than chase it.

**Internal linking — bigger problem than the summary suggests**

I crawled all 163 sitemap URLs and counted in-content links only (header, nav and footer stripped). Several of SPIEDR's best pages get no internal support at all:

- `/roof-sprinkler-system/` — **zero** in-content inbound links, yet ranks #12–21 for six roof sprinkler terms worth about 460 searches/month combined. Clearest opportunity on the site, and it's behind one of the real declines.
- `/fire-embers/` — **zero** inbound links, and it's the site's top organic traffic page (#5 for "what is embers").
- `/mark-3-pump/` — only 2 inbound links, and it's the page behind the "portable fire pumps canada" #1 that just started slipping.
- `/spiedr-fire-pump-protection-systems/` — 1 inbound link.
- 45 of 93 blog posts have zero in-content inbound links and are reachable only via a ten-page paginated archive. That includes the BC fire bans post, which ranks #25–31 for "fire ban bc" (5,400/mo) and "bc fire ban" (3,600/mo) — the largest impression pool on the domain, with nothing linking to it.

I've mapped the specific source pages for each — in every case the anchor text is already sitting in the body copy, so these are edits, not new content.

**Suggested order:** roof sprinkler page first, then Mark-3 pump (defends the slipping #1), then fire embers, then the fire pumps page. Blog hub restructure after that.

**One flag on the campaign itself:** 44 of the 47 tracked keywords have 0–30 monthly searches, which is what generates most of this Top 3 / Top 10 churn. Meanwhile the terms SPIEDR genuinely ranks for at volume aren't tracked at all. I'd like to refresh the Position Tracking keyword set before the next report.

Also worth noting for the report template: Position Tracking here is Desktop / British Columbia only, while the GSC section is national. Those two will never agree, and we should say so where they sit side by side.
