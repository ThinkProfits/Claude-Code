# Industry page template (thinkprofits.com `/industries/<slug>/`)

> **Reusable component.** Use this for every ThinkProfits industry page (plumbing, HVAC, law firms, dental, hotels, ecommerce, manufacturing, home services, education).
> **Pilot:** [plumbing/plumbing-page-draft-v1.md](plumbing/plumbing-page-draft-v1.md) is the worked example. Copy its structure, not its wording.
> **Lovable prompts:** [plumbing/lovable-build-plumbing.txt](plumbing/lovable-build-plumbing.txt). Swap the URL, the case study links and the section count.
> **Research behind it:** [../industry-pages-audit-2026-10-07.md](../industry-pages-audit-2026-10-07.md). Keyword targets per industry are in section 8, the FAQ bank in section 9, and demand in section 3.
> **Owner:** Francis (SEO). Last updated 2026-10-07.

## Rules for every industry page

1. **One page per industry** covering SEO, AI search and Google Ads. No separate AEO or PPC pages per industry.
2. **Written to the business owner** hiring an agency. Customer phrases appear only as short, labelled examples.
3. **Proof or nothing.** Every number comes from our own case studies, published pricing or a cited source. If there's no proof, there's no claim.
4. **No guarantees.** Not on rankings, AI citations or timelines. Describe the process; give ranges with stated assumptions.
5. **Canadian English and Canadian terms** (lawyers / Law Society, licence, neighbourhood, CAD, GST). The US is a secondary market.
6. **Written by hand.** Claude may consolidate a draft; Francis rewrites and Andrew approves. Lovable only builds and pastes.
7. **Visual style** = `/ppc-advertising/ppc-for-plumbing/` (the layout the team approved on 2026-10-07).

|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hero | H1 | Primary keyword plus a hook. 6 trust badges. Button to /contact/ and the phone number. |
| 2 | Lead form | H2 | Budget and need selects tailored to the industry. Turnstile. |
| 3 | Proof strip | — | 3–4 real numbers, each linked to a case study. |
| 4 | Why us for this industry | H2 | The industry-specific credibility story (for plumbing: the Moore family history and video). Use whatever is genuinely true for that industry: client tenure, sub-sector depth, regulatory know-how. |

| 7 | How their customers choose | H2 + 4×H3 | The buying journey for *their* customers, in 4 steps. |
| 8 | Results | H2 | 2–4 case study cards with real numbers and real attributed quotes. |
| 9 | What's included | H2 | Checklist of 8–10 items. |
| 9a | Why choose us | H2 | Ownership · month-to-month · real people · published pricing · industry depth · writers. |

## Per-industry checklist (before drafting)

- [ ] Primary and secondary keywords from audit section 8 (re-check volumes in Keyword Planner if more than 3 months old).
- [ ] People Also Ask / competitor FAQ questions pulled (standing rule).
- [ ] Case studies chosen; permission to name each client confirmed.
- [ ] Industry-specific credibility story agreed with Shawn or Andrew.
- [ ] Regulatory rules for advertising in that industry checked (law, dental, health).
- [ ] Old URLs for that industry listed for the redirect map (audit section 7).
- [ ] Unsourced stats from the old templated pages stripped.

## Quality check (before sending to Andrew)

- [ ] Keyword in the title, H1, first paragraph and slug.
- [ ] Every number traced to a source (listed in the draft's "Claims to confirm").
- [ ] Banned phrases absent: "368,000", "Reddit threads", "training and retrieval set", "Q&A seeding", guarantee wording.
- [ ] Canadian spelling.
- [ ] Internal links spelled out (anchor + URL table).
- [ ] Service-page format (scannable sections, no blog-style explainer H2s).
