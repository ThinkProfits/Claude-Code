### 7. How homeowners choose a plumber

## H2: How Homeowners Actually Find a Plumber

### H3: 1. The Emergency
Water on the floor and a phone in one hand. They call the first listing that loads fast and shows a phone number. Winning here means a fast mobile site, correct 24/7 hours on Google and Local Services Ads.

### H3: 2. The Symptom Search
"Why is my water pressure low?" "Toilet won't stop running." Long before they search "plumber", they search the problem. Helpful, honest answers on your site put your name in front of them first, and feed Google's AI answers too.

### H3: 3. The Map Pack Shortlist
"Plumber [city]." "Drain cleaning near me." Three map listings decide the shortlist. Reviews, categories and a page for every city you serve decide who's on it.

### H3: 4. The Big-Ticket Decision
Repipes, sewer lines and tankless installs get researched for weeks. These buyers want scope, warranties, financing and proof you've done the job before. We build those pages properly, and they quietly become the highest-value pages on your site.

### 11. FAQ

## H2: Plumbing Marketing FAQs

### H3: How much does SEO for plumbers cost?
Our plumbing SEO programs start at $995/month and go up to $4,995/month for multi-city companies, plus setup. Google Ads management starts at $399/month plus your ad spend. The right mix depends on your market and goals, and we'll recommend a range on a free call.

### H3: How long does plumbing SEO take to work?
Google Business Profile fixes can show movement within a few weeks. Breaking into the top three map listings in a competitive city usually takes several months of steady reviews, citations and city pages. Google Ads and Local Services Ads can start producing calls as soon as they're approved, so most plumbers run both while SEO builds.

### H3: Should I use SEO, Google Ads or Local Services Ads?
Usually all three, for different jobs. Local Services Ads and Google Ads bring calls now. SEO builds rankings you own, which get cheaper per call over time. We help you set the split based on your budget and how busy your trucks are.

### H3: Can you help me get the Google Guaranteed badge?
Yes. We handle the Local Services Ads setup, including licence and insurance verification and background checks, and we manage budgets and dispute invalid leads afterwards.

### H3: How many Google reviews does a plumber need?
There's no magic number. It depends on how many reviews your local competitors have and how recent they are. A steady flow of new reviews often beats a big but stale total. We set up a simple system to ask every happy customer.

### H3: Is SEO better than buying leads from lead marketplaces?
Lead marketplaces can fill gaps, but the same lead is often sold to several plumbers, and the calls stop when you stop paying. SEO and your own ads bring calls that come to you alone.

### H3: How can a local plumber compete with franchises?
Franchises have big budgets, but their pages are often templated across hundreds of locations. Independent plumbers win with real local detail, more recent reviews, and pages for the neighbourhoods they actually serve.

### H3: Can you get my plumbing company recommended by ChatGPT?
No one can guarantee what an AI tool will say. What we can do is make your business easy for AI tools to understand and cite: clear service pages, a consistent business listing everywhere, helpful FAQ answers and mentions on trusted sites. We track where you show up each month.

### H3: Do you work with my competitors?
*(Confirm current policy.)* We work with one plumbing company per service area, so we're never building rankings for the shop down the street.

### H3: Do I have to sign a long-term contract?
No. All our programs are month-to-month, and you keep ownership of your website, ad accounts and content.
