The rule with source "/blog/:path+" (destination /services/) is near the top of redirects[], at about #30. Because the first match wins, every more specific /blog/... rule after it never fires. That includes all /blog/case-studies/*, all /blog/testimonial/*, the old blog posts mapped to /digital-news/... articles, and the wildcard rules "/blog/case-studies/:slug" and "/blog/testimonial/:slug".

Fix: move the "/blog/:path+" rule so it sits AFTER every other rule whose source starts with /blog/. Keep it as the final fallback. Don't change its destination.
