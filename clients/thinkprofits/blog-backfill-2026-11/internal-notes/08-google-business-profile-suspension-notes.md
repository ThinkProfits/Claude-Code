## Claims cut or reframed as unverifiable

- "3-5 days" review time and a "60-minute evidence upload window" appeared in search snippets but were NOT on the fetched Google pages. Cut; post uses only "up to five business days" from 13597551.
- "Only two chances to reinstate" (Search Engine Land, third-party). Cut.
- Reported multi-week delays (Search Engine Journal, BrightLocal 2022). Not cited; post only says "some businesses report longer waits" and advises planning for a week offline. Editor may cut that sentence if it feels unsourced.
- Hard vs soft suspension terminology: not in Google's docs, not used.
- Signage photos: brief listed them as Google reinstatement evidence, but Google's appeal pages don't list photos. Post labels photos "our addition" and ties them to the guidelines' signage/real-world-proof language.
- Canadian document mapping (BC Registries, municipal licence, CRA GST/HST confirmation, hydro bill) is our practitioner mapping of Google's generic categories, not a Google statement. Worded accordingly.
- Removed a draft claim about "Suite 210" vs "#210" being fine (unverified) and any "most wins we've seen" experience claims.
