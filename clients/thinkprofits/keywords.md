## 6. Known keyword/page issues

- **Cannibalisation:** `/seo-services/` vs `/seo-company-vancouver/` vs `/services/`; `/web-design-services/` vs `/vancouver-web-design-services/`. The 2026-09-14 weekly audit also flagged `/seo-services` and `/seo-services/` serving different titles/copy (slash conflict). *Confirm fixed.*
- **Trailing slash variants** show up as separate GSC rows (e.g. `/digital-news/how-long-should-blog-post-be-seo-ai-2026` with and without `/`).
- **Lost content:** clicks dropped from mid-August after the Lovable migration; some old blog posts and testimonials weren't migrated (team meeting 2026-08-25). See `data/_blog_triage.csv` (237 old Kinsta URLs: 45 KEEP / 119 MAYBE / 73 DROP).
- **PAA research** has not been run for these clusters yet (standing rule: `memory/paa-research-standard.md`).
