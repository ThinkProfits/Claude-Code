## 4. Brand-safety and out-of-scope flags

**Adult-intent visibility.** Semrush (US) shows rankings for "sensual massage maui" (590/mo, #23–26), "erotic massage maui" (170, #20), "happy ending massage maui" (90, #26), "nuru massage maui" (90, #23), "maui nuru massage" (40, #30). GSC shows actual clicks on "erotic massage maui", "maui erotic massage", "sensual massage maui", "adult massage maui", "happy ending massage maui" and "body rub near me". All rank on the homepage.
- Risk: misaligned leads, safety risk for mobile massage therapists visiting private rentals, and reputational risk.
- Actions: never target these; keep them as Ads negatives if PPC restarts; check homepage copy and reviews for phrasing that could be feeding the association; consider a clear professional-boundaries line on the booking/FAQ page. *Discuss with Andrew/Shawn before any copy change.*

**Out-of-area rankings.** The lomilomi page ranks for Oahu/Waikiki/Honolulu terms (5 keywords, positions 6–61). Harmless traffic-wise but it dilutes local signals. Add Maui-specific copy and don't chase these.

**Services not offered that show up:** thai massage maui (#44), maui yoga and massage (#43), "massage parlor maui" (#28), "maui asian massage" (#34). Don't target. *Confirm yoga isn't part of the retreats offer.*

**Irrelevant:** "aloe massage" (390, #26; likely misspelling or another brand) and "makai massage and bodywork" (competitor brand).

1. **"mobile massage maui" / "maui mobile massage".** Core terms, positions 2–3 in Semrush US but 18–28 average in GSC. The gap suggests location-dependent rankings. A cleaner homepage (one H1, one business schema, no cannibalizing service pages) is the cheapest lever.
2. **"maui massage" (1,900/mo, #31) and "massage maui hawaii" (480, #38).** The only real head-term volume on the island. Needs homepage relevance plus location pages.
3. **Location pages for priority areas.** Kihei (~390 ×3 variants, already ranking 14–28), Wailea (110 + variants, 19–41), Lahaina (170 + variants, 16–35) and Kapalua (40–50, not ranking). Build on the new site.
4. **Lymphatic drainage.** The blog already ranks #2–5 and drives 62 clicks/90 days. A dedicated service page (if offered) plus internal links from the homepage.
5. **"massage near me" (277 impressions, pos 10.7) and "mobile massage near me" (117, 12.8).** Depend on GBP proximity. Keep GBP categories and services tight.
6. **Couples / wedding / group.** "couples massage maui" (480, #38) is a meaningful term, but copy must respect the side-by-side rule.
7. **New services at rebrand.** Pull Maui volumes for mobile facial, microneedling, waxing and "mobile spa maui" before the new site's IA is finalized.
