**Flags (check against CLIENT.md §2)**
- **Member-school brand terms** (13, e.g. "bastyr university", "national university of natural medicine", "canadian college of naturopathic medicine"): AANMC ranking for its members' names is fine, but never optimise to outrank a member's own site. *Confirm the client's view; consider tagging these as "member brand" and reporting them separately.*
- **Patient/local intent** (10, e.g. "naturopathic doctor", "naturopath", "naturopathist", "nd doctor", "natural medicine doctor"): mostly people seeking care. Rankings here are trust/visibility signals, not lead drivers.
- **"american association of naturopathic physicians":** partner organisation's name; navigational. Don't target.
- **"university of natural health", "phd in natural medicine":** non-ND / possibly non-accredited credentials. Content should steer to accredited ND education, never endorse.
- **"dangers of naturopathy"** (480/mo, #4 via /holistic-medicine-benefits-risks/): sensitive. Keep the answer balanced and sourced.
- **"best ... schools"** terms: fine to track; page copy must explain criteria (accreditation) rather than claim superiority.
- Multiple URLs win similar terms (e.g. "become a naturopathic doctor" → homepage, "how to become a naturopath" → /featured-articles/long-take-become-nd/, "become a naturopath" → /naturopathic-news/become-licensed-naturopathic-doctor/).
