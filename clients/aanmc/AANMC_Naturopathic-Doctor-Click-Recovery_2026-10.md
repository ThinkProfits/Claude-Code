# Naturopathic Doctor Keywords: Why Clicks Dropped and How We Win Them Back

## The Short Version

AANMC still ranks for "naturopathic doctor" terms, but fewer people click. Five things are causing it:

- **No page owns the term.** At least five AANMC pages compete for "naturopathic doctor", so Google rotates between them and none of them wins.
- **Most searchers aren't the audience.** People typing "naturopathic doctor" mostly want to book one nearby. Google answers them with a map of local clinics before any organic result.
- **Google's own answers sit on top.** The map pack, AI answers and "People Also Ask" boxes push every regular listing down the page.
- **The listings don't sell the click.** Key pages have missing or off-topic meta descriptions, generic titles and no visible "last updated" date.
- **Demand dipped a little.** Semrush's trend line for "naturopathic doctor" is about a fifth to a third lower in recent months than earlier in the year. Some of the impression drop is the market, not the site.

Three of these we can fix directly: cannibalization, weak listings and missing answer content. The local-intent and AI-answer effects we can't remove, but we can work around them.

### What the results page looks like now

Semrush's US results page for "naturopathic doctor" (40,500 searches/month, difficulty 68):

- **Local pack first:** three clinic listings (dmdnaturalmedicine.com, naturopathicgroup.com, naturalmeddoc.com).
- **Organic #1:** naturopathic.org's "What is a Naturopathic Doctor" page.
- **Organic #2:** aanmc.org/naturopathic-medicine/.
- **Then:** NIH (NCCIH), Bastyr, the AMA, Cleveland Clinic and NUHS.

So AANMC holds a strong organic spot, but on a phone it sits below a map, three clinics and usually an AI answer. That explains why Search Console's average position (13.2) is so much worse than the organic rank. Mobile carries 78% of the impressions (53,118 of 67,832) and gets a 0.07% click-through.

## The Plan

### 1. Give "naturopathic doctor" one owner page (highest priority)

**Target keyword:** what is a naturopathic doctor (2,400/mo US) + naturopathic doctor (40,500/mo US)
**Search intent:** informational, with prospective students and curious patients mixed together

Create a new page at **/what-is-a-naturopathic-doctor/**. Don't repurpose /comparing-nd-md-curricula/: it currently ranks #2–3 for "naturopathic doctor vs md" and "is a naturopathic doctor an md", and we shouldn't put that at risk.

Recommended outline, built from Semrush question data:

- **H1:** What Is a Naturopathic Doctor (ND)?
- **Answer-first block (40–60 words) right under the H1.** A plain definition of a licensed ND: accredited four-year doctoral program, board exams (NPLEX), licensed by state or province. This is the passage AI answers and featured snippets pull from.
- **H2: What Does a Naturopathic Doctor Do?** (260/mo + variants)
- **H2: Are Naturopathic Doctors Real Doctors?** (480 + 390 + 390 + 260/mo across variants — the biggest question cluster). Answer factually: what NDs train in and where their scope stops.
- **H2: Naturopathic Doctor vs Traditional Naturopath** → short summary, linking to the full article
- **H2: Naturopathic Doctor vs MD** → short summary, linking to /comparing-nd-md-curricula/
- **H2: Can Naturopathic Doctors Prescribe Medication?** → short summary, linking to /can-naturopathic-doctors-prescribe-medication/
- **H2: Where Are Naturopathic Doctors Licensed?** → summary, linking to /licensure/
- **H2: Does Insurance Cover Naturopathic Doctors?** (110/mo; Medicare variant 170/mo). Factual and short, with a "check your plan and state" note.
- **H2: How to Find a Licensed Naturopathic Doctor** (110/mo). Point to the AANP and CAND directories and state/provincial boards.
- **H2: How to Become a Naturopathic Doctor** → CTA to /naturopathic-schools/ and /naturopathic-news/become-licensed-naturopathic-doctor/
- **FAQ block** (4–6 questions from the list above)
- **Byline:** "Medically reviewed by [ND name, credentials]" plus a visible "Last updated" date

Once it's live, de-optimize the competitors so Google stops rotating:

- Change sitewide internal anchors that say "naturopathic doctor" to point at the new page.
- Narrow /naturopathic-medicine/ to "naturopathic medicine" (see section 2).
- Narrow the traditional-vs-licensed article to the "naturopath vs naturopathic doctor" intent (see section 2).

### 3. Capture the patient searchers instead of fighting them

Most "naturopathic doctor" and "nd doctor" searches are people looking for care. AANMC can't win the local map, and shouldn't try. But it can be the trusted page those searchers land on when they ask "is this person legit?"

- **Add a "How to Find and Verify a Licensed ND" section** on the new owner page and on /licensure/. Link out to state and provincial licensing boards, and to the AANP and CAND directories (both already referenced on /licensure/).
- **Turn /licensure/'s bullet lists into a table:** state/province | licensed? | board link. Tables get lifted into AI answers more readily than bullets.

**Client decision needed:** is a patient-facing section on mission for AANMC? It serves the profession and builds trust links, but it isn't student recruitment.

### 4. Get cited in AI answers (GEO/AEO)

- Open each priority page with a short, quotable definition (done in sections 1–2).
- Add named, credentialed reviewers and visible update dates. AI systems and Google both weigh who wrote the content and how fresh it is.
- Keep facts consistent across pages: program length, number of accredited schools, licensed jurisdictions. Contradictions reduce how often a site gets cited.
- Schema: see Implementation Notes.

### 5. Internal links from the traffic you already have

AANMC's 2026 growth comes from consumer-health articles (oil of oregano, natural antibiotics, dandruff). Those pages get thousands of clicks a month but don't feed the core topic. Add one contextual line to each: "Want guidance from a licensed professional? Learn [what a naturopathic doctor is](/what-is-a-naturopathic-doctor/)."

## How We'll Measure It

Monthly Search Console review of the naturopathic-doctor cluster:

- Clicks and click-through for: naturopathic doctor, nd doctor, what is a naturopathic doctor, naturopathic doctor salary, naturopathic medical school.
- Count of aanmc.org URLs getting impressions for "naturopathic doctor". The goal is consolidation onto the new page.
- Position of /what-is-a-naturopathic-doctor/ for its question cluster.

Realistic timing: title and meta changes usually show up within 2–6 weeks of re-crawl. A new page typically needs 2–4 months to settle. Results vary, and the local-intent share of these searches puts a ceiling on how far "naturopathic doctor" clicks can recover.
