## 2. Services: what they do and don't do

**What they do** (residential)
- **Hot water:** tank installs and replacement; tankless installs, repair and maintenance, especially **Navien**
- **Heating:**
  - Boilers, including combi: installs, repair and maintenance
  - Furnaces: installs, repair and maintenance
  - In-floor / radiant heating
- **Heat pumps and AC:** installs. Repairs are **new as of August 2026**; early stage, not pushed hard yet.
- **Gas fitting**
- **Strata maintenance** for residential buildings up to 4 storeys (treated as residential)
- **Navien:** Navien Certified Service Specialist (NSS). Handles all Navien residential lines **except water treatment**.
- **Brands serviced:** Navien, Lennox, Carrier, Trane, Rheem, American Standard, Napoleon, Continental, Goodman, Keeprite, Bradford White, AO Smith, Giant, John Wood

**What they don't do**
- General plumbing repairs (faucets, toilets)
- Drain cleaning (subbed out)
- New construction
- Propane or oil work, including conversions
- Fire installations. *Confirm how this squares with the "Gas Fireplaces & Outdoor Fire Pits" menu item.*
- Commercial work (stratas are the exception)

**Regulatory**
- TSBC licences: **LGA0092486** (gas), **LBP0207908** (plumbing)
- **Any ad mentioning gas services must show the gas licence number.** This is a legal requirement. The Client Overview writes it as "#92486"; the full number is LGA0092486.

- Colin checks technical facts, then Tina approves. **Never publish without sign-off.**
- Approvals are slow. In November 2025 the client committed to reviewing 2 items a week. Andrew's guidance (2026-02-19): keep writing, and leave approval with the client.

**Brand rules (hard)**
- **No AI-generated images or fake personas, ever.** The client is concerned about "AI slop."
- No discounts, gimmicks, fake urgency or inflated-then-discounted pricing. Anniversary promos should be community/charity-focused.
- No em dashes.
- No "#1" or "best" superlatives.
- FAQ schema answers carry no links and no branded language.

**Voice:** "we/our", grade 8–9 reading level, local family business. Trust signals over hype. Lead with Navien expertise.

**Formatting**
- Quick-answer blocks use the "Preformatted" style.
- FAQs use the same FAQ plugin as Munro & Crawford.

**Keyword routing**
- Navien queries go to the /services/navien/ hub.
- Track Navien at category level (e.g. "navien error codes"), not per error code.
- Zero-volume niche terms count as an AEO opportunity.

**Topics to avoid:** general plumbing repairs, drain cleaning, commercial HVAC, propane/oil, new construction.
