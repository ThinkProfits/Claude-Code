Frequently Asked Questions

Are your plumbers licensed?

Yes. All John Sadler plumbers hold current TSBC licences (#LGA0092486, #LBP0207908) and are Red Seal certified.

What areas do you cover?

Surrey, Langley, White Rock, North Delta, and the surrounding Lower Mainland communities listed above.

What's your typical response time for a service call?

Most calls are handled same-day.

Do you offer emergency or after-hours plumbing?

Yes — we offer 24/7 emergency plumbing service for urgent issues like leaks, burst pipes, or no heat/hot water.

What brands do you work with?

We service and install all major hot water tank, boiler, and furnace brands — including Rheem, AO Smith, Bradford White, Giant, John Wood, and Navien — not just the ones we sell.

Are you certified to work on Navien systems?

Yes. John Sadler is a Certified Navien Service Specialist (NSS), factory-trained on Navien combi boilers and tankless water heaters.

Do you install tankless water heaters?

Yes — installation, replacement, and repair for tankless systems, alongside conventional tank water heaters.

Do you offer maintenance plans for strata or multi-unit buildings?

Yes, we provide ongoing plumbing and heating maintenance contracts for strata councils and multi-unit properties.
