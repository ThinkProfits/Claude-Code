## How to Choose a Reliable Plumber Near You

![John Sadler Plumbing & Heating TSBC licence](https://www.johnsadler.ca/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/TSBC-Licence.png)

Not everyone who shows up with a wrench is a licensed plumber. In BC, look for three things before hiring:

1. **A valid TSBC (Technical Safety BC) licence number** — legally required to be displayed on the company's website and materials since 2023. Ours: #LGA0092486 and #LBP0207908.
2. **A written estimate before work starts** — not just a verbal quote.
3. **Proof of insurance** — ask directly; a licensed company will have it on hand.

Choosing based on price alone is the most common mistake homeowners make — the cheapest quote often comes from an unlicensed contractor, which can void manufacturer warranties on new equipment.
