Q: Should I replace my furnace before selling? A: If it is over 15 years old or needs frequent repairs, replacing it can head off an inspection flag. If it is newer and serviced yearly, a tune-up and records are usually enough.

Q: Do I need a permit for a new gas furnace in BC? A: Generally yes. A licensed gas contractor applies for the Technical Safety BC permit on your behalf.
