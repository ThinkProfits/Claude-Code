## Hard rules (client + agency)

- No em dashes or en dashes anywhere. Canadian spelling (colour, centre, licence (noun), litre).
- No "#1", "best", "top-rated" or other superlatives. No discounts, urgency or "free estimate" (estimate policy unconfirmed). No 24/7 or emergency claims (unconfirmed; one live page wrongly says 24/7).
- "We/our" voice, grade 8–9 reading level, local family business, trust signals over hype. Lead with Navien expertise where it fits naturally.
- No competitors named or linked. No US programs, bodies or stats. Canadian/BC sources only (NRCan, Technical Safety BC, FortisBC, CleanBC/BC Hydro, Health Canada, BC Building Code, manufacturer docs).
- Rebates: amounts and eligibility change often (FortisBC changed programs Dec 2025). Do NOT quote dollar amounts. Point readers to the client's /fortisbc-rebates/ page or the program site and say to confirm current eligibility.
- No prices or cost figures.
- FAQ answers: no links and no branded language (they feed FAQ schema).
- Quick Answer block: client uses the WordPress "Preformatted" style for this. In the draft, mark it as a blockquote and label it "Quick Answer (Preformatted block)".
- Every statistic needs a source link. Verify every external URL loads (WebFetch) before using it. If you cannot verify a fact, leave it out.
- Safety: any gas smell, CO alarm or scorching = leave the home / call FortisBC emergency line (1-800-663-9911) or 911. Verify that number on fortisbc.com before using it.
