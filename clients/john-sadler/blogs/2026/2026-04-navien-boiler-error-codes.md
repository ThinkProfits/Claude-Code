# Navien Boiler Error Codes Explained: NFB-H and NHB-H

> **Quick Answer (Preformatted block):** Navien NFB-H and NHB-H boilers show an "E" code on the front panel when they detect a problem, such as E003 (ignition failure), E012 (flame loss) or E302 (low water pressure). Navien's manuals say to try a reset first by pressing the Back button. If the code comes back, the boiler needs a licensed technician to find the cause, and if you smell gas, leave the home and call FortisBC at 1-800-663-9911 or 911.

## Gas Safety Comes First

Some situations call for leaving the house, not looking up a code.

- **If you smell gas (a rotten egg odour):** don't light anything, touch switches or use a phone inside; go outside. FortisBC lists its 24-hour emergency line as 1-800-663-9911, and you can also call 911, as outlined on its [gas leaks and odour page](https://www.fortisbc.com/safety-outages/energy-safety/natural-gas-safety/gas-leaks-and-odour).
- **If your carbon monoxide alarm sounds:** get everyone outside and call 911.

Everything below assumes there's no gas smell and no alarm. Gas valves, burners, venting and wiring inside the boiler are for a licensed gas fitter only.

## How to Read and Reset a Navien Boiler Error Code

The code appears on the boiler's front panel as the letter E followed by three digits. Navien's user manuals for both series give the same first step: press the Back button on the front panel to reset the boiler, then see whether the code returns.

A single code after a power blip often clears with one reset. A code that comes back, or a boiler that locks out again a few hours later, means something hasn't been fixed, so one or two resets is the reasonable limit. Before you call, write down the code, your model number (for example, NFB-175H or NHB-110H) and the serial number from the rating plate, since Navien's manuals ask for the same details.

## Ignition and Flame Codes: E003, E004 and E012

These codes mean the boiler either couldn't light or couldn't keep its flame. They are among the most common codes we see, especially at the start of the heating season.

- **E003, Ignition Failure:** the boiler tried to light and didn't detect a flame. Navien's action is to check the gas supply valve and igniter, so confirm the gas valve to the boiler is open and that your other gas appliances, like the stove or water heater, are working.
- **E004, False Flame:** the boiler detected a flame signal when there shouldn't be one. Navien points to the electrical ground, which is a technician check.
- **E012, Flame Loss:** the boiler lit but lost its flame during operation. Navien lists gas, venting and the condensate trap as the places to look.

The condensate trap matters more than most people expect. Condensing boilers produce water as they run, and a clogged or frozen condensate line can make the boiler lose its flame. You can check that the drain line isn't blocked or frozen outside, but the trap and venting are technician jobs.

## Overheat and Venting Codes

These codes mean the boiler is running too hot or can't move exhaust out properly, usually because of poor water flow or a venting problem.

- **E001 and E016, Heat Exchanger Overheat:** the heat exchanger got too hot. Navien's actions are to check for proper system flow and clean the system filters, which often points to a stuck zone valve, an air-locked loop or a failing circulator pump.
- **E030, Exhaust Overheat:** the exhaust temperature is too high. Navien again lists flow and system filters as the first checks.
- **E031, Burner Overheat (NFB-H):** this code appears in the NFB-H tables and points to the burner temperature fuse.
- **E109, Abnormal Fan Motor:** the combustion fan isn't running at the right speed. Navien says to check the fan and venting.
- **E110, Exhaust Blockage:** the boiler has detected a blocked exhaust. Check that the outside vent and intake terminals are clear of snow, leaves or nests.
- **E762, Vent Pipe Not Fully Inserted:** the vent pipe isn't fully seated in the exhaust collar, an installation issue for a gas fitter.

If E110 or E030 returns once the outside vents are clear, the problem is inside the venting or the boiler.

## Water Pressure and Flow Codes

A boiler moves heated water through a closed loop of pipes, radiators or in-floor tubing, and it needs steady pressure to do that safely. These codes flag a problem in that loop.

- **E302, Low Water Pressure:** system pressure has dropped too low. Navien's action is to check water pressure, and the boiler's information menu can display the current reading.
- **E352, High Water Pressure:** pressure is too high, which can point to an expansion tank or fill valve issue.
- **E353, Water Pressure Sensor:** the pressure sensor itself isn't reading correctly.
- **E777, Low Water Cutoff Activated:** the low water cutoff has shut the boiler down. Navien says to check the supply water.
- **E291, Return Temp. Exceeds Supply:** the return water is hotter than the supply water, and Navien's action is to check for reversed piping.

Low pressure that keeps coming back usually means water is escaping through a small leak, a weeping relief valve or a tired expansion tank. Look for drips under the boiler and along visible piping, but leave the relief and fill valves alone unless your installer has shown you how to use them. ## What You Can Safely Check, and When to Call

Four quick checks either clear the code or help the technician arrive prepared.

1. **Reset once.** Press the Back button and note whether the same code returns, and how quickly.
2. **Check power and the gas valve.** Make sure the boiler's switch and breaker are on and the gas shut-off valve to the boiler is open (the handle runs in line with the pipe).
3. **Check the outside vents and condensate line.** Clear snow or debris from the vent terminals and look for a frozen or blocked condensate drain.
4. **Note the pressure.** If the display shows a pressure reading, write it down for the technician.

Call for service when a code returns after a reset, the boiler locks out repeatedly, or an overheat, venting or pressure code doesn't clear once the outside vents are clear. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I reset a Navien boiler?

Press the Back button on the boiler's front panel. That clears the current error so the boiler can try to restart. If the same code appears again, the underlying problem still needs to be found and fixed by a licensed technician.

### What does error code E003 mean on a Navien boiler?

E003 means ignition failure: the boiler tried to light but didn't detect a flame. Common causes include a closed gas valve, a gas supply interruption, a blocked condensate drain or a worn igniter. If it returns after one reset with the gas valve open, book a service call.

### Why does my Navien boiler keep showing low water pressure?

Code E302 means system pressure has dropped below the safe operating range. Pressure that keeps dropping usually means water is escaping through a small leak, a weeping relief valve or a problem with the expansion tank. A technician can find the source and bring the system back to the correct fill pressure.

### Can I fix a Navien boiler error code myself?

You can safely reset the boiler, confirm the power and gas valve are on, and clear snow or debris from the outside vents. Repairs inside the cabinet involve live wiring and sealed gas components, so in BC that work belongs with a licensed gas fitter.
