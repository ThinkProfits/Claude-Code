# Navien Tankless Water Heater Maintenance: What's Involved and How Often

> **Quick Answer (Preformatted block):** Navien recommends maintenance on its tankless water heaters once a year, including descaling (flushing) the heat exchanger at least annually, and more often with hard water, high temperature settings or heavy use. Between visits, Navien's manual asks owners to clean the air intake filter every 3 to 4 months. A full service also covers the inlet water filter, condensate trap, venting and a final operating check.

A Navien tankless water heater is built to run for years with very little attention, but "very little" is not the same as none. Navien tankless water heater maintenance comes down to a short list of jobs: flushing mineral scale out of the heat exchanger, cleaning a couple of filters, checking the condensate and venting, and confirming the unit is firing correctly. Skip them and the first signs are usually error codes or hot water that runs lukewarm.

This guide explains how often each job needs doing according to Navien's own manuals, what our technicians do during a service visit in Surrey, BC, and how servicing differs for Navien combi boilers, boilers and the hydro-furnace.

## How Often Does a Navien Tankless Water Heater Need Maintenance?

Navien's answer is once a year for a full service, plus one quick owner task every few months.

Navien's guidance on [descaling tankless water heaters](https://www.navieninc.com/blog/notice/how-to-descale-tankless-water-heaters) recommends descaling at least once a year. It adds that the right frequency depends on the mineral content of your water and how high the temperature is set, and it recommends having a professional do the work.

The [Navien NPE-2 User's Information Manual](https://www.navieninc.com/downloads/npe-2-user-s-information-manual-en/download) sets out the rest of the schedule:

- **Every 3 to 4 months:** clean the air intake filter. If it clogs with dust or lint, the manual says the unit stops running and shows error code 110.
- **Once a year:** inspect the vent termination and air intake supply to confirm they are clear, sealed and undamaged.
- **When the display shows E760:** this is Navien's flushing and service alarm, and the manual's instruction is to contact your installer for routine service.

## What's Involved in a Navien Tankless Service Visit

A Navien tankless service follows the order laid out in Navien's manual. Here is what our technicians do, and why each step matters.

### 1. Shutting Down and Draining the Unit

The technician turns the unit off, closes the gas and water supply valves, and drains the water still inside. Navien's manual calls for draining before any filter cleaning or flushing.

### 2. Flushing (Descaling) the Heat Exchanger

This is the main event. Using the isolation valves on the hot and cold lines, the technician connects a small pump and circulates a descaling solution through the heat exchanger for at least 45 minutes, then rinses it with fresh water. Navien's manual describes flushing as "a somewhat complicated procedure" and points owners who are unsure to an authorized technician or licensed professional.

### 3. Cleaning the Inlet Water Filters

Every Navien tankless has a small screen on the cold water inlet that catches sediment before it reaches the heat exchanger. The technician removes it, rinses it under cold running water and scrubs it if needed. Navien "A2" models with built-in recirculation have a second screen on the recirculation inlet, which gets the same treatment.

### 4. Cleaning the Air Intake Filter

The air intake filter keeps dust and lint out of the blower and burner. It is cleaned with a soft brush and running water, then left to dry fully before it goes back in.

### 5. Checking the Condensate Trap and Drain

Condensing water heaters produce acidic condensate, which drains through a trap at the bottom of the unit. The technician checks the trap and drain line for blockages and makes sure the trap's o-ring is seated properly when it is reinstalled, a detail Navien calls out specifically.

### 6. Inspecting the Venting and Air Intake

The technician checks the vent and intake pipes for damage, separated joints and blockages outside. Navien's manual says to stop using the unit if any vent pipe shows corrosion, rusting or melting.

### 7. Restarting and Verifying Operation

Once everything is reassembled, the unit is refilled, powered on and test-fired. Navien's maintenance guidance says to verify operation, including gas pressure, when putting a unit back into service. That check is gas work, and in BC it should be done by a licensed gas fitter.

## Does Surrey's Soft Water Mean You Can Skip Descaling?

Surrey's water is low in minerals, but Navien still recommends descaling every year.

Most Surrey homes are on the regional water system, and [Metro Vancouver's 2025 Water Quality Annual Report](https://metrovancouver.org/services/water/Documents/water-quality-annual-report-volume-1-2025.pdf) shows treated water hardness averaging roughly 2 to 21 mg/L (as calcium carbonate) across the Capilano, Seymour and Coquitlam systems. That is low, which means scale tends to build slowly here compared with many parts of Canada.

Slow is not zero, though. Navien's descaling interval does not change for soft water, and the inlet filter, venting and condensate need checking regardless of water quality. If your home is on a private well, your water may be very different, so it is worth testing.

## What You Can Do Between Service Visits

A few owner tasks keep a Navien running smoothly between annual visits:

- **Clean the air intake filter every 3 to 4 months.** It is the one job Navien's manual expects owners to do regularly, and it prevents the E110 error that shuts the unit down.
- **Keep the area around the unit clear.** Navien's manual warns against storing laundry, paper, sprays or anything flammable near the water heater or its vent.
- **Leave the power and gas on during cold snaps.** The built-in freeze protection needs electricity to work, and Navien's limited warranty does not cover freeze damage.
- **Note any error codes.** Navien's troubleshooting chart links codes like E016 (heat exchanger overheating) to a dirty inlet filter or a heat exchanger that needs flushing. Write the code down before calling, as it speeds up the diagnosis.

## Servicing Other Navien Equipment: Combi Boilers, Boilers and the Hydro-Furnace

The tankless routine carries over to other Navien equipment, with a few differences.

### Navien Combi Boilers

A combi boiler heats your home and your tap water, so it gets both sets of checks. The [Navien NCB-H User's Information Manual](https://www.navieninc.com/downloads/ncb-h-user-s-information-manual-en/download) says the boiler must be inspected annually, preferably at the start of the heating season, by a qualified service technician. That annual start-up covers the condensate trap, leak checks, venting, system pressure and expansion tank, ignition and flame, plus flushing the heat exchanger and cleaning the filters when performance calls for it. The same manual asks owners to look over the condensate trap and vent piping monthly. Learn more about how these units work on our [combi boiler](https://www.johnsadler.ca/combi-boiler/) page.

### Navien Boilers (NHB and NFB)

Navien's NHB-H and NFB-H manuals say these boilers should be inspected annually by a qualified service technician, using the same start-up checklist as the combi boiler. Our post on [annual boiler service](https://www.johnsadler.ca/blog/annual-boiler-service/) covers what that visit looks like on a typical system. If your boiler is showing a code, our guide to Navien boiler error codes (link once published: /blog/navien-boiler-error-codes/) explains the common ones.

### The Navien NPF Hydro-Furnace

The hydro-furnace's manual lists a monthly air filter check and a check of the blower, combustion chamber, intake filter, water level and venting at the start of each heating season. If a condensate neutralizer is installed, Navien recommends an annual pH test. ## Maintenance and Your Navien Warranty

Regular maintenance does more than keep the hot water flowing. It also protects your warranty.

Navien's NPE-2 manual lists "failure to perform regular maintenance" among the conditions that void its limited warranty, and it excludes damage caused by hard water deposits or mineral build-up. It also cautions that improper maintenance can void the warranty, which is a good reason to think twice before a do-it-yourself flush. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### How often should a Navien tankless water heater be serviced?

Navien recommends annual maintenance, including descaling the heat exchanger at least once a year. Homes with hard water, high temperature settings or heavy hot water use may need service more often. The air intake filter should also be cleaned every 3 to 4 months between visits.

### How do I know my Navien tankless needs to be flushed?

The clearest sign is error code E760, which is Navien's flushing and service alarm. Codes such as E016 (heat exchanger overheating) and E030 (exhaust overheating) also point to a dirty inlet filter or scale in the heat exchanger. Lukewarm water or reduced hot water flow can be an earlier warning.

### Can I flush a Navien tankless water heater myself?

It is possible with a pump, hoses, a bucket and a descaling solution, but Navien's manual describes it as a somewhat complicated procedure. Navien recommends hiring a professional, because damage caused by improper maintenance can void the warranty. Cleaning the air intake filter is the owner task Navien expects you to handle.

### Does skipping maintenance affect the Navien warranty?

It can. Navien's limited warranty lists failure to perform regular maintenance as a condition that voids coverage, and it excludes damage caused by hard water deposits or mineral build-up. Keeping a record of each annual service helps if you ever need to make a claim.
