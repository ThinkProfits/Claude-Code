## How these numbers were pulled (read first)

- **Volume:** Google Keyword Planner, **geo-targeted to Calgary** (geo 1001801), 12-month average (Oct 2025 to Sept 2026). Estate and transition terms were also pulled for **all of Alberta** (geo 20113), because Calgary-only volume was too small to read.

- **Keyword Planner groups close variants.** "calgary realtor" (14,800) includes "realtor calgary" and "realtors in calgary alberta". Treat a grouped figure as shared demand, not demand for each term.

## 2. Which fourth page: Buying or Estate & Transition Sales?

**Recommendation: build Estate & Transition Sales. Keep About. Cover buying on Home (plus short mentions on Selling and About).**

Why:
1. **Buying terms are portal territory and attract the wrong clients.** "buy a house calgary" (1,600) and the "homes for sale" terms are owned by realtor.ca, Zolo, Wahi and CREB. A Buying page would mostly draw first-time buyers in newer districts, which is the group she said she doesn't want.
2. **Estate & Transition has almost no search volume, but almost no competition, and it's her edge.**
   - None of her seven named competitors has an estate, probate, executor, power of attorney or assisted-living page (section 4).
   - For "selling a parent's house under power of attorney in Alberta", **no Calgary agent ranks at all.** Results are banks, BC sites and US law firms.
   - A lawyer writing about who has authority to sell is exactly the expertise Google and AI answers reward.
3. **It converts the traffic she already has.** Almost all her enquiries come from her law client database. That page is the obvious place to send those clients and is the landing page for the Estate Options cross-link.
4. **It's the best Google Ads landing page** for estate and transition terms later, since low organic volume doesn't mean low ad value.
5. **Is it enough for its own page?** Yes on content (authority to sell, probate timing, Land Titles, EPA, capacity, downsizing all need real explanation). No on volume alone. We're recommending it for conversion, differentiation and AI visibility, not traffic. Say this plainly to her.

**Why not drop About for both?** Trust has to carry this site. She has no testimonials yet and won't post sales numbers, so her story, legal background and teaching *are* the proof. About also answers "is it a conflict for my realtor to be a lawyer?". A Buying page can be phase two if buyer enquiries grow.
