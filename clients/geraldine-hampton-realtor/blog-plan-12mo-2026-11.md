- **KD and Semrush question data: not available this round.** Every Semrush keyword_research report (phrase_these, phrase_kdi, phrase_questions, phrase_organic) returned 'no API units left'. Nothing was charged. KD for landing-page keywords is in the tracking list (from the approved sheets); blog KD is blank until units are topped up.
- **PAA questions:** Google autocomplete question suggestions (google.ca, English), pulled 2026-11-24, used as the People Also Ask proxy because Semrush phrase_questions was unavailable. Re-check the live PAA box when each post is briefed and fold the questions into its FAQ (memory/paa-research-standard.md).
- **Volumes are small on purpose.** These are local, niche topics. The posts are for conversion, AI answers and internal links to the service pages, not traffic. Results build over months; nothing here is a ranking promise.
