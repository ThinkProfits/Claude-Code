## How the numbers were pulled

- **Volume:** Google Keyword Planner, Calgary (geo 1001801) unless marked AB (Alberta, 20113) or CA (Canada, 2124). Landing-page volumes come from the **APPROVED keyword sheet** (Drive, approved by Geraldine 2026-10-29, Oct 2026 pull). New keywords were pulled 2026-11-24 (12-month average, Nov 2025 to Oct 2026). Keyword Planner groups close variants: a grouped figure is shared demand.
- **KD:** Semrush keyword difficulty as recorded on the APPROVED sheet. "pending" = not on that sheet; the Semrush API had no units left on 2026-11-24 (every keyword_research report returned "no API units left"), so KD couldn't be pulled. Position Tracking shows KD once keywords are added.
- **Intent:** from the approved sheet where given; otherwise our call.
- **Target URL:** the live page that should rank (live sitemap checked 2026-11-24).
