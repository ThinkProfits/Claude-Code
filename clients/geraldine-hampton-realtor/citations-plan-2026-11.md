2. **Brokerage address vs. service-area business.** Many directories show the street address publicly. Using the RE/MAX office is normal for agents and matches her contact page, but:
   - Google's guidelines allow an agent's GBP at the brokerage only if she is staffed there during stated hours. `[NEEDS: confirm with Andrew whether Geraldine works from the RE/MAX office or we list her as a service-area business]`
   - **Never use her home address** (standing rule in the 2026-10 research).
   - If she goes service-area: tick "hide address" / "service area only" where offered, and skip directories that force a public street address.
3. **P.O. boxes are rejected by most directories** (Google, Bing, Apple, Yelp, Yellow Pages and most aggregators require a street address). Don't submit the P.O. box anywhere for the realtor.
4. **Hours: 7 days, 9 am to 10 pm** is unusually long. It matches her site, so keep it consistent, but confirm it's what she wants public.
5. **Name with ®:** some forms strip the symbol. Use "Geraldine Hampton, REALTOR" without the symbol rather than inventing a different name ("Geraldine Hampton Real Estate", "Geraldine Hampton RE/MAX", etc.).
6. **Don't cross-link the law practice.** One email address (hers) is shared between the two businesses in some places. Use only gh@geraldinehampton.com here.
