### SEO Strategy

> Fix the local service pages first (cannibalization, duplicate city content, gaps), then build authority. Near-page-one terms should move first; "managed it services vancouver" (about 1,300 searches/month) will take longer. Silver has no ongoing link building, so authority closes slowly.

> - **Scope:** 1 tracked area (Vancouver), 2 blogs/month. Ian makes about 4 videos a month, and the blog slots can flex to video scripts (agreed November 2).
> - **Tracking locations:** Vancouver is the Semrush position tracking area, so keyword research and volume checks should use Vancouver local data. BrightLocal map grids are centred on Burnaby (the office).
> - **Protect Burnaby:** Adaptive already ranks well around Burnaby (page one for "managed it services burnaby"). When consolidating or rewriting location and service pages, check current Burnaby rankings first and don't remove or retarget pages that hold them.
> - **GBP:** optimize the linked landing page, categories and services. **Hard rule: no borrowed or virtual addresses for extra locations.** Ian: "I'm not going to cheat." Report keyword-stuffed competitor names instead.

> - **Target:** managed IT / IT support / IT company + each city; LTC, law and accounting vertical terms; switching-provider intent; cybersecurity, Microsoft 365, AI consulting.
