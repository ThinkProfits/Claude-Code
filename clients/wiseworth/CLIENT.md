## 8. Content guidelines

**Blogs:** none in scope. /blog/ and /company-news/ exist on the site.

**GBP posts**
- **Images:** real images only, at least 400×300, taken from the site (building/storefront, `wiseworth-about.jpg`, dryer photo). No AI-generated or fabricated images.
- **Links:** verify every CTA URL exists before sending. The site is behind Cloudflare, so link checks need a full Chrome user agent.
- **Rentals:** never promote them.

**Voice:** professional, technical and spec-focused, for an industrial B2B buyer. Lean on safety, uptime, quality and IR expertise ("Seeing is believing"). Skip the consumer cutesy stuff; a dry, competent wit is fine.

**Content boundaries**
- No rentals.
- No ecommerce/"buy online" language.
- Don't run down Ingersoll Rand or claim to be IR.
- Product copy usually comes from the client. Placeholder "coming soon" sections are long-standing and waiting on client content.
