## Issues

**The last 30 days are down sharply on last year** (GSC)
- **Clicks 390 vs 700 (-44%)**, impressions 31,584 vs 45,578 (-31%).
- **Most of the drop is Canada and brand searches, not lost rankings:**
  - Canada clicks **52 vs 254 (-80%)**; US clicks 249 vs 310 (-20%).
  - Searches for the Mujo name fell from about 240 clicks to 42 ("mujo learning" 94 to 0, "mujo learning systems" 88 to 23, "mujo" 44 to 18). That's about two-thirds of the lost clicks.
  - The homepage, where brand searches land, fell 236 to 71 clicks while its position held (4.9 to 5.2). The Canadian Google Business Profile link (homepage with a `gmb_canada` tag) fell 43 to 2 clicks.
  - **Semrush confirms the ranking held:** Mujo is still #1 in Canada for "mujo" (210 searches/month).
  - Over 24 Jul to 21 Nov, Canada overall fell from 402 to 212 clicks (-47%).
  - **Likely cause (to confirm with Brittni):** fewer people searching for Mujo by name in Canada this fall, e.g. a Canadian school or college course that used Mujo last fall and doesn't this term. SEO can't win back searches that aren't happening, so this is worth a direct question.

**Teaching, lesson-plan and curriculum searches slipped**
- Semrush tracked keywords (May 23 vs Nov 23):
  - "how to teach SEO": 1 to 38
  - "social media marketing lesson plans for high school students": 1 to 32
  - "how to teach digital marketing": 1 to 17
  - "marketing curriculum high school": 1 to 7
  - "college textbook for digital marketing": 5 to 48
  - "digital marketing essentials textbooks": 3 to 23
  - "teaching social media marketing": 9 to 31
  - "marketing automation syllabus": 2 to 76
  - Dropped out of the top 100: "teaching digital marketing" (was 8), "social media curriculum" (20), "teaching marketing in high school" (23), "marketing automation curriculum" (23), "curriculum digital marketing" (41)
- GSC agrees:
  - /high-school/digital-marketing-curriculum/: 99 vs 171 clicks (24 Jul to 21 Nov), impressions 5,656 vs 19,578. Position is fine (11.3 to 7.9); it's showing for fewer searches.
  - "digital marketing lesson plans", "social media marketing curriculum high school" and "web design lesson plans for high school" no longer bring clicks. Low volume (about 10 US searches a month each, per the October review), but they're teacher-intent searches.
- **Pattern:** product and textbook searches went up; teaching how-to and curriculum searches went down. The blog and curriculum pages need the next round of work.

**Seen a lot but clicked rarely**
- **AI for Business (high school): 10,362 impressions, position 5.9, but only 28 clicks (0.27% CTR)** in the last 6 months (GSC). Still the biggest quick win on the site; the title and meta description rewrite hasn't been done yet. Semrush shows it also ranks for an irrelevant query, which inflates impressions.
- **Homepage clicks down (320 vs 578, 24 Jul to 21 Nov)** while impressions held (24,450 vs 22,269), mostly the Canadian brand drop. Brand-name collision with other "Mujo" products keeps CTR low ("mujo" 0.67% CTR on 22,548 impressions; "mujo ai" 0.58% on 7,996).

**Not ranking yet**
- 35 of the 70 tracked keywords don't rank in the top 100, including the biggest: "digital marketing fundamentals" (210/month), "social media for educators" (70/month) and the "best digital marketing textbook(s)" group.
- "ai textbook" (320/month) is at #8 with the high school product page, not the AI Textbooks hub.

**Tracking gap**
- Semrush tracking misses the AI and business catalogue: only 6 of 81 US tracked keywords and none of the 70 Canadian ones cover AI or business titles, so the monthly report doesn't show the AI wins above.

**SEO decisions waiting on Mujo**
- High School / Higher Education wording in titles and H1s; product consolidation (separate student and teacher products); whether the Shop page should be indexed; current proof points (250+ schools etc.) and the ADA claim; whether local SEO / Google Business Profiles are paused or active.
