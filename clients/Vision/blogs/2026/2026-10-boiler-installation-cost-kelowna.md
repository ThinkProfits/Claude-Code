## The Boiler Itself: Type, Efficiency and Size

The equipment you choose sets the floor for your boiler installation cost.

### Combi vs. Heat-Only Boilers

A combi boiler heats your home and heats domestic water on demand, so it can replace both your boiler and your hot water tank. That usually makes the combi boiler cost lower than a heat-only boiler plus a separate indirect tank. The trade-off is capacity: in a larger home with several bathrooms running at once, a heat-only boiler with an indirect tank often keeps up better. [VISION TO CONFIRM: brands Vision typically installs and any preferred combi line.]

### Condensing vs. Non-Condensing

Most new gas boilers for homes are high-efficiency condensing units, which pull extra heat out of the exhaust before it leaves the house. If you're replacing an older non-condensing boiler, expect added work: condensing boilers vent through plastic pipe instead of a chimney and produce acidic condensate that needs a drain and usually a neutraliser. They also run most efficiently with cooler return water, which suits in-floor heating well.

### Size and Heat-Loss Sizing

A boiler should be sized to your home's heat loss, not to the nameplate on the old unit. Copying an oversized boiler forward means a pricier unit that short cycles, wears faster and wastes gas. A proper [heating load calculation](https://visionplumbingandheating.com/all-about-hvac-load-calculation/) looks at insulation, windows, square footage and the output of your existing baseboards or in-floor loops. [VISION TO CONFIRM: how Vision sizes replacement boilers and whether it's part of the assessment.] Right-sizing often means a smaller boiler and a lower equipment cost.

## Installation Work That Moves the Number

These four items explain why the same boiler can cost very different amounts once installed.

### Venting and Condensate

Moving from a chimney-vented boiler to a condensing model means new plastic venting out a side wall or roof, plus a condensate drain line. If there's no floor drain nearby, a small condensate pump may be needed. In a finished basement, routing that venting cleanly is often the biggest labour variable in the job.

### Gas Line Work

A new boiler may need a different gas pipe size than the old one, especially a combi boiler that fires harder for hot water. Longer runs or a [new gas line installation](https://visionplumbingandheating.com/residential/kelowna-gas-line-installation/) for other appliances at the same time add to the quote.

### Removing the Old Boiler

Older cast-iron boilers are heavy, and some sit in spaces built around them. Removal covers draining the system, disconnecting gas and venting, and hauling the old unit away. [VISION TO CONFIRM: whether removal and disposal are included in the standard install price.]

### Hydronic Distribution Changes

The boiler is only half of a hydronic system; the other half is the piping, pumps and baseboards or floor loops that deliver heat. Some installs reuse everything as-is, while others need new circulator pumps, zone valves, an expansion tank or a system flush to clear sludge before it reaches the new heat exchanger. Adding in-floor heat to a basement becomes a [hydronic heating installation](https://visionplumbingandheating.com/residential/plumbing/hydronic-heating-installation-kelowna/) in its own right, and that's where hydronic heating cost climbs fastest.

## Permits for Boiler Installation in Kelowna and West Kelowna

Replacing a gas boiler requires a gas permit, and who issues it depends on where you live. Technical Safety BC lists [installing or replacing a boiler among the gas work that needs a permit](https://technicalsafetybc.ca/apply-for/permits/homeowner-permits/homeowner-gas-permits), and notes that a licensed contractor applies on your behalf.

Kelowna is one of a handful of BC municipalities that [issues its own gas installation permits](https://www.technicalsafetybc.ca/permits/gas-installation), so inside city limits the permit goes through the City of Kelowna. In West Kelowna and Lake Country, gas permits come from Technical Safety BC. Either way, the permit fee is part of your boiler installation cost, and skipping it can cause problems with insurance and resale. ## The Boiler Installation Process, Step by Step

Here's what a typical boiler replacement looks like from first call to first heat.

1. **In-home assessment.** A technician looks at your current boiler, venting, gas supply and hydronic piping, and asks how much hot water your household uses. This is where sizing, combi vs. heat-only and any distribution changes get decided.
2. **Written quote.** You receive a written quote that spells out the boiler model, the installation work and what's included. Once you approve it, the equipment is ordered and an install date is booked.
3. **Permit application.** As the licensed contractor, Vision applies for the gas permit with the City of Kelowna or Technical Safety BC, depending on your address. You don't need to file anything yourself.
4. **Removal and preparation.** On install day, the crew shuts off gas and power, drains the system and removes the old unit. They then prepare the mounting space, venting route and condensate drain.
5. **Installation.** The new boiler is mounted and connected to gas, venting, water, electrical and the hydronic piping, along with any new pumps, valves or controls. If the system needs flushing, it happens before the new boiler is filled.
6. **Commissioning and walkthrough.** The technician purges air from the system, checks combustion, tests for gas leaks and confirms every zone heats. They also review your [carbon monoxide safety](https://visionplumbingandheating.com/carbon-monoxide-safety-checks/) setup, show you the controls and arrange the permit inspection.

A like-for-like swap usually takes [VISION TO CONFIRM: number] day(s); jobs with new venting or distribution changes take longer.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does a boiler installation take?

A like-for-like boiler replacement with existing plastic venting is usually completed in [VISION TO CONFIRM: number] day(s). Converting a chimney-vented boiler to a condensing model, adding zones or flushing a sludged system can add a day or more.

### Is a combi boiler cheaper to install than a boiler and separate water heater?

Usually, yes, because one appliance replaces two and there's less piping and venting to install. In larger homes with high hot water demand, a heat-only boiler with an indirect tank can be the better fit even though it costs more upfront.

### Do I need a permit to replace a boiler in Kelowna?

Yes, replacing a gas boiler requires a gas permit. Inside Kelowna city limits the City issues gas permits, while in West Kelowna and most other BC communities they come from Technical Safety BC. When a licensed gas contractor does the work, the contractor applies for the permit.

### Can a new boiler use my existing baseboards or in-floor heating?

In most cases, yes. Existing baseboards, radiators and in-floor loops can usually be reconnected, though older systems may need flushing, a new circulator pump or updated controls. Condensing boilers run most efficiently at lower water temperatures, so in-floor systems tend to benefit most.
