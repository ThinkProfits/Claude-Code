# Water line jobs: photo and video checklist for technicians

> **For:** Vision technicians on trenchless and underground water line jobs. Written to be handed to the crew as-is. Vision-facing, so: no competitor names, no em dashes.
> **Part of:** [water-main-roadmap-2026-10-27.md](water-main-roadmap-2026-10-27.md) (Phase 2, real-job content). Internal notes for Francis and Andrew are at the bottom.
> **Reusable:** the shot list and the job notes card work for any service. Swap the water line details for the job type.

---

## Why this matters

When someone asks Google or an AI assistant "who replaces water lines in West Kelowna?", the answer depends on proof: photos, real projects and reviews that mention the work and the town. Vision does this work every week. Most of it never makes it online. Five minutes of phone photos per job fixes that.

## The 60-second version

1. **Before:** 3 photos of the problem.
2. **During:** 3 photos and 1 short video of the work.
3. **After:** 2 photos of the finished job.
4. **Notes:** fill in the job card (below).
5. **Send:** upload to the job in the CRM, or to the shared folder, the same day.

## Shot list

Take everything **horizontal (landscape)** unless it says video. Wipe the lens first. Daylight beats flash.

### Before (3 photos)
- [ ] **The problem:** the leak, wet patch, sinkhole, burst pipe or old line where it's exposed.
- [ ] **The old pipe close up:** show the material (Poly-B, copper, galvanized, black poly) and any damage or corrosion.
- [ ] **The site wide shot:** the driveway, lawn or landscaping we're about to avoid digging up. This is what makes "trenchless" make sense to a homeowner.

### During (3 photos + 1 video)
- [ ] **Equipment set up:** bursting or pulling rig, boring machine, the access pits. A Vision truck or branded gear in frame is a bonus.
- [ ] **The new pipe going in**, or the new pipe laid out ready to go.
- [ ] **Access pit with crew working**, everyone in proper PPE.
- [ ] **Video, 20 to 40 seconds, vertical:** a tech explains what's happening in plain words. Example: "We're pulling a new line under this driveway so the homeowner doesn't lose their pavers. Two small holes instead of a trench." No script needed. Face on camera is great but optional.

### After (2 photos)
- [ ] **Same wide shot as before**, from the same spot: driveway or lawn still intact. The before-and-after pair is the most useful image we get.
- [ ] **The new connection or meter area**, finished and tidy.

### Bonus (only if it happens naturally)
- [ ] The old pipe pulled out, laid next to the new one.
- [ ] A short video of the homeowner saying what they thought, **only with their OK** (see Privacy).

## Job notes card

Send this with the photos. Short answers are fine.

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| Date | 2026-11-12 |
| Town | West Kelowna |
| Neighbourhood or street name (no house number) | Shannon Lake area |
| Property type | Single-family house |
| What the homeowner noticed | Wet patch in the front lawn, low pressure, high water bill |
| Old pipe material and age if known | Poly-B, about 30 years |
| New pipe material | (whatever was installed) |
| Method | Pipe bursting / pulling / directional boring / open trench |
| Length of line | About 25 m |
| What we avoided digging up | Stamped concrete driveway and mature trees |
| Time on site | 1 day |
| Water off for | About 6 hours |
| Anything unusual | Rock at 1 m, city shut-off seized, etc. |
| Homeowner OK to use photos? | Yes / No |
| Homeowner OK to use their first name and town in a quote? | Yes / No |

## Privacy and what not to shoot

- **No house numbers, street signs next to the house, or licence plates** in frame. Crop or retake.
- **No people's faces** (homeowners, neighbours, kids) unless they say yes.
- **Nothing inside the home** unless the homeowner agrees.
- **No unsafe-looking scenes:** missing PPE, unshored pits, crew in the road without traffic control. If it wouldn't pass an inspection, don't post it.
- **Ask, don't assume:** "Mind if we take a few photos of the work for our website? No address or faces." Most people say yes.

## Asking for a review

At the end of a job that went well, the tech or the office asks for a review through Podium as usual. One extra line helps:

> "If you leave us a review, it really helps if you mention what we did and what town you're in, like 'replaced our water line in Peachland'."

- **Do:** ask every happy customer, and ask for honest detail.
- **Don't:** write the review for them, offer a discount or gift for it, or ask only the happy ones to post on Google while sending unhappy ones somewhere else. All of these break Google's review rules and can get reviews removed.

## How to send

- **Best:** attach photos, video and notes to the job in the CRM, so they're tied to the job record.
- **Otherwise:** the shared Google Drive folder for water line jobs (*folder to be set up*).
- **File names**, if uploading to Drive: `YYYY-MM-DD_town_street_before-1.jpg`. Example: `2026-11-12_west-kelowna_shannon-lake-rd_after-1.jpg`.
- **Same day** if possible. Photos sitting on a phone for a month tend to stay there.

## What happens to the photos

ThinkProfits turns them into:
- A short project write-up on the website (town, problem, method, result)
- A Google Business Profile post on the Kelowna or Vernon profile
- Photos on the trenchless water line page and the town pages
- Social posts Vision can schedule

The office gets a draft to approve before anything goes live.
