## 1. Headlines

1. **Organic rankings are strong for the core term.** Vision is #1 organically for "water line replacement" + Kelowna, Penticton and Vernon. One generic page carries all of that.
2. **Maps is the gap.** Vision isn't in the 3-pack for water line searches in any of the 7 towns. Its only Maps showing is #3 for "poly b replacement kelowna". This is the biggest lever for Brent's goal.
3. **Search volume is small and spread thin.** Every water main term is about 10 searches a month or less across the Okanagan towns. Town-name variants ("water line replacement penticton") show zero volume. This is a long-tail category with high-value jobs. Any lead projections should be modest ranges.
4. **Clicks are expensive.** Keyword Planner top-of-page bid ranges (BC) run about $6–36 for trenchless terms and $12–44 for "water line repair" and "poly b replacement kelowna".
5. **Poly-B is where the volume is.** BC volumes:
   - "poly b": 590/mo
   - "poly b replacement": 140/mo
   - "poly b pipe replacement": 110/mo

   Vision has **7 pages** splitting about 8,300 Poly-B impressions, and the best page sits around position 20. Most of this demand is indoor repiping, not the underground service line Brent means.
6. **AI and "recommend me" searches favour King Rooter and Crystal Clear.** In Facebook groups, Yelp and the Maps packs for "who replaces underground water lines" style queries, Vision doesn't appear.
7. **The trenchless page has accuracy problems** that need fixing before any campaign points traffic at it (section 5).

**What the numbers say**
- Brent's list is the right *topic* list, but most of his phrasings have no measurable search volume on their own. Treat them as page sections, FAQs and ad-group themes, not as separate ranking targets.

- Town pages are worth building for Maps relevance, ad landing pages and AI "who serves X" answers, not for town-keyword volume.
- With demand this thin, owning the category mostly means Maps + Ads impression share + review and third-party signals. Expect lead counts in the low tens a month across all channels, not hundreds. That is an assumption to firm up against Vision's actual job value and close rate from Housecall Pro, and it needs Andrew's Ads data before any figure goes to Brent.

## 5. Problems on the current trenchless page

Fix these before the page gets more traffic. Wording changes need Andrew's check.

1. **It says Vision does trenchless sewer.** The page text includes "trenchless replacements for sewers, water mains, water lines". The FAQ also asks "Do you handle trenchless sewer line replacement too?" (the answer wasn't checked). Vision doesn't do trenchless sewer (CLIENT.md section 2).
2. **CIPP relining is described as a water line method.** The meta description also says "Pipe bursting & CIPP relining". CIPP is mostly a sewer method. *Confirm with Vision* that they reline water services. If not, remove it.
3. **Leftover copy from other pages:**
   - "No hidden condo-access surcharges"
   - "Live dispatch for no-heat or no-cool crises"
4. **Claims to confirm:**
   - "Same Day Service"
   - "service restored the same day"
   - "half the time of traditional"
   - "rated to last 50 years or more"
   - "quality-guaranteed"
5. **No case studies, project photos or town-specific content.** That is what competitors also lack, and what Brent asked for.
