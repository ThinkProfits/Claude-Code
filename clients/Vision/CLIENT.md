## 2. Services: what they do and don't do

**What they do** (residential and commercial)
- **Heating:** furnace installation, repair, maintenance and rejuvenation packages; emergency furnace repair; boilers (repair and install); hydronic/radiant in-floor heating; gas fireplace service and repair
- **Cooling:** AC installation, repair and maintenance; central AC; ductless mini-splits (all heat pumps; the difference is form factor); heat pump installation, repair and maintenance. Bryant dealer; AirEase products (buy-back promo March 2026).
- **Plumbing:** general plumbing service and repairs, emergency plumbing (24/7), clogged drains and sewer line services (hydro-jetting, drain camera inspections, conventional sewer line repair), toilets and faucets, hot water tanks, tankless water heaters
- **Water lines and repiping:** trenchless water main / water line replacement ("line pull"), Poly-B and Kitec replacement, general repipes (~15% of the business, 2023)
- **Leak detection and flood prevention:** Sedna/Sinope smart shut-off valves ("biggest dealer in Canada for Sedna by Sinope", 2023)
- **Water treatment:** softeners, filtration, UV (added 2024)
- **Indoor air quality:** expanding in 2026 to Dynamic Air whole-home systems (UV, electrostatic filters, optional HEPA); IAQ monitors used on in-home visits (Slack 2026-08-18)
- **Gas fitting:** gas lines (BBQ/patio lines blogged), licensed gas fitters
- **Commercial:** plumbing service, emergency plumbing, HVAC installation and maintenance, rooftop units; hotels, apartments, strata, restaurants, offices, property managers
- Programmes: **Vision Elite Membership** (replaced the Elite Service Plan, 2026-01-30): 10% off, priority scheduling, no dispatch fees. Senior discount (65+). Financing up to $100,000.

**What they don't do**

## 8. Content guidelines

**Approval flow:** Francis drafts, then Andrew edits, then Vision approves. **Never publish without client approval.** Drafts go in the client's Drive (Vision > Jobs > SEO > Blogs (year)) before being linked in the masterlist.

**Andrew's standing rules** (2025-11 to 2026-09)
- **Local first:** Kelowna, West Kelowna and Okanagan specifics. No US programmes, regulators or sources (use NRCan, not DOE). This is the #1 rule.
- **Length:** 3–5 pages, not 12–16.
- **One Quick Answer block**, at the top, in the established style (match /septic-safe-plumbing-west-kelowna-westside-road-casa-loma/).
- **FAQs:** factual and unbiased. No brand mentions or internal links in answers. H2 "Frequently Asked Questions" with H3 questions (the Make pipeline depends on this).
- **CTAs:** "contact us" or "in-home assessment". **No "free consultation/estimate"** and no "no upsells, no pressure".
- **Rebates:** genericise to "available rebates" with a link to /financing-rebates/ in most blogs. Name FortisBC/CleanBC only where it's the point. Don't say Vision handles rebate paperwork.
- **Accuracy:** mini-splits are heat pumps; R-454B/R-32 for new systems; furnace/HVAC filters are not standalone air filters; Vision doesn't do generators or ducts.
- **Never contradict annual maintenance** messaging.
- **Don't publish full step-by-step checklists** (e.g. the 26-point AC checklist) unless Vision confirms they do every step.
- **No competitor promotion.**
- **Tone:** professional; no informal AI tells ("given up on life", one-line "Hold on.") and no em dashes on service pages.
- **Links:** verify every internal URL against the sitemap and live-check government URLs (2026-09-17). Anchor text must match the target page.
- **Phone:** use 250-470-9552, never a tracking number.
- **Images:** **no AI images** (credibility risk; one had a Gemini logo). Use the media library, Vision's Drive folders or GBP photos, or ask Vision. Setting the featured image already displays it, so don't add it again in the body.
- **Table styling:** brand colours, black on orange, with legible contrast.
- **Process:** check the site's past blogs for tone, service areas and cannibalisation before writing; fact-check; re-prompt AI for a review until it stops finding errors; read it yourself before sending (2026-05-09).
- PTAC FAQ wording to use: "Do you install PTAC units? No, and they often can't be repaired either. We instead offer upgrades to ductless split systems." (2026-08-22)

**Topics to avoid:** trenchless sewer, ventilation/HRV, ductwork, sump pumps, thermostats, generators, sprinkler winterizing, garbage disposals, bathroom fans, DIY guides that push readers away from hiring Vision.
