## What the numbers mean for Ads

- **Volume is tiny:** about 10 searches a month per core term across the Okanagan; town-name variants show 0. Expect low impression counts. Broad or phrase match with tight negatives will probably be needed to serve at all.
- **Clicks are expensive:** top-of-page bids in BC run about $6–44. "water line repair" and "poly b replacement kelowna" are at the top ($12–44).
- **Poly-B has the volume** (BC: poly b 590, poly b replacement 140, poly b pipe replacement 110), but most of it is indoor repiping. There's already a Poly B ad group (spiked in Aug). Decide whether underground service line ads sit with it or apart.
- **Existing campaign to check first:** TP - Repiping. Water line terms may already be in there.

## Negatives to consider

- **Sewer (Vision doesn't do trenchless sewer):** sewer, sewer line, septic, drain, lateral, CIPP, relining, pipe lining, storm
- **Other services:** gas line, irrigation, sprinkler, pool, well, well pump
- **Jobs and DIY:** jobs, careers, salary, course, training, DIY, how to, rental, rent, machine, equipment for sale
- **Out of territory:** Kamloops (GSC shows Kamloops impressions), Salmon Arm, Osoyoos, Oliver. Check against where Vision actually travels
- **"pipe bursting" on its own** is mixed with sewer. Use it only with the sewer negatives above in place
