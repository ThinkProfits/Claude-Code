## Recommendation

**Do the foundation work inside the current package, and add AEO Bronze for the off-site and AI work.** Reasons:
- **What wins this category isn't more SEO.** Vision already ranks #1 organically in 3 towns. The gaps are Maps, reviews, third-party mentions, case studies and AI answers. That's AEO work, not more of what the SEO package already does.
- **Bronze fits the size of the market.** Search volume is about 10 a month per term in the Okanagan, so a big monthly add-on would be hard to justify on the numbers. Bronze covers exactly 2 services, which is what Brent is asking about.
- **It lets the existing SEO hours stay on HVAC and plumbing.** Without an add-on, this work displaces furnace and heating content during peak season.
- **Silver is the step up** if Brent wants all 6 towns covered and 2 case studies a month inside 90 days. Offer it as the faster option, not the default.

**Pick Silver if** Brent wants every town covered inside 90 days. Pick Bronze if he's fine with the 3 bigger towns first and the smaller ones in months 4–6. Either way, case studies depend on Vision's techs sending photos. No package speeds up jobs that aren't captured.
