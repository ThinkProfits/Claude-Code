# Owning underground water line searches across the Okanagan

**Prepared for:** Brent, Vision Plumbing Heating Cooling
**Prepared by:** ThinkProfits
**Date:** October 2026

## The short version

- **Good news first:** Vision's trenchless page already sits at the top of Google's regular results for "water line replacement" in Kelowna, Penticton and Vernon.
- **The gap is Google Maps.** For water line searches, Vision doesn't show in the top 3 map results in any of the 7 towns. Those 3 spots get the calls, so that's where most of this plan is aimed.
- **Fewer people search for this than you'd think.** Google's keyword data shows about 10 searches a month per term across the Okanagan. These are high-value jobs, but there aren't many of them, so every search counts.
- **Proof wins this category.** Google and AI assistants like ChatGPT recommend the companies with the most visible proof: real project photos, reviews that mention water lines, and mentions on other sites. Vision does this work every week. Most of it just never makes it online.
- **The plan:** fix and strengthen the page that already works, get real job proof online in every town, and track results per town every month.

- **The trenchless page needs a cleanup** before we send more traffic to it. It mentions trenchless sewer work, which Vision doesn't offer, and has some copy left over from other pages.

## 2. What other companies in the area are doing

We looked at who shows up for water line searches in each town.
- **The companies winning the Maps spots have a local address** in that town and reviews that mention the exact service ("replaced our water line under the driveway").
- **A few have a page for each town.** Most of those pages are thin.
- **Almost none show real projects** with photos, the pipe material and the town.
- **Almost none have pages on specific pipe materials** (Poly-B, copper, galvanized, black poly).

**Vision's opening:** 40 years in business, 700+ reviews and multiple Best of Kelowna awards already beat most of the companies in those Maps spots. What's missing is tying that reputation to water line work, town by town.

## 6. AI search (ChatGPT, Google's AI answers and others)

AI assistants build their answers from what other sites and customers say about a business. To show up there, Vision needs:
- **Reviews that say what was done and where.** For example: "Vision replaced our water line in West Kelowna."
- **Up-to-date profiles on the sites AI tools read,** such as HomeStars, Yelp, BBB and Facebook, with water line replacement listed as a service.
- **Real local mentions,** for example supplier dealer listings, local news coverage of a notable job, and community sponsorships.
- **Clear, direct answers on the website** to common questions, which the guide and FAQ in section 4 cover.

We'll track a set of 8 everyday questions (like "who does trenchless water line replacement in Kelowna?") every month and report whether Vision is mentioned.

## 7. Photos and videos from your team

This is the part only Vision can do, and it makes the biggest difference. We've put together a one-page checklist for the crew:
- 3 photos before, 3 during, 2 after, plus one short video (20 to 40 seconds) of a tech explaining the job.
- A quick job card: town, street name (never the house number), pipe material, method, and what we avoided digging up.
- Privacy built in: no house numbers, licence plates or faces without permission.
- A simple line for asking happy customers to mention the job and town in their review.

**What we need from you:** one person on your team who makes sure each water line job gets its photos and job card. In our experience, it usually drops off after a few weeks without someone owning it.
